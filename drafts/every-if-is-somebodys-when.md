# When an IF Becomes Somebody's WHEN

## 🦅 TL;DR

**A person's “I will be able to X, if” has another side: “we will be able to
offer X, when”.** Put the two sentences together. Find the condition, then
find who holds it. That gives the next conversation a place to start.

This is a working frame, with [evidence and authority boundaries](../boundaries.md)
and [capture provenance](../reviews/round-08-narrative-provenance.md) alongside it.

## 🔀 Turn the sentence around

The [first piece](name-the-if.md) starts with the person receiving the
promise. They want to do something. Price, permission, proximity,
availability and capability prompt the search for what that requires.

Now turn towards the offering side. In the conversation that developed this
frame, the sentence became:

> “I, as an enterprise, will be able to offer fill in the blank ... when I can
> do this and this and this.”

That is the list for the CEO. It is the list for the entrepreneur. The promise
becomes a set of conditions to examine and work on. **Every demand-side if
points towards somebody's supply-side project.** Following that connection
reveals another question, and then another.

The two registers are linked but non-identical. A provider might solve a
capacity problem while the person still faces a journey or a bill. Completing
the provider's list is useful only in relation to the outcome the person
actually needs. Keep that outcome in view as the work becomes more detailed.

Here is a worked pair of sentences:

> **Consumer:** I can get the consultation if the cost is within reach, the
> appointment is usable and the route is permitted here.
>
> **Provider:** We can offer that consultation when the necessary capacity,
> funding and permission are in place.

These are illustrative applications of the captured grammar. The useful
detail is what gets filled in: which consultation, for whom, in which place,
to what standard and within what time. **The same words can hide very
different conditions until the scope is explicit.**

## 🧭 Who holds the condition?

Some conditions sit inside an organisation. Some depend on the technology.
Others live between organisations, in their contracts and commitments. That
distinction came out when the frame was applied to software development:

> “We will be able to ... unleash the AI to develop the next generation of
> products if the quality of the models is trustworthy enough” and the
> verification, compliance and review requirements are met.

This is a shortened quote from the [8 September conversation](../capture/2026-09-08-abundance-frame-conversation.md),
kept generic while the employer-naming question remains open.

The first reading might put all those conditions under “internal process”.
Look again. A process could express a customer commitment. A review could
serve an external requirement. Model quality could be the unresolved part.
**Naming the condition starts the investigation; tracing its owner makes the
investigation useful.**

An internal preference invites one conversation. A technical limitation
invites another. A term held by a counterparty calls for a conversation with
that counterparty. The method helps keep those conversations from being
collapsed into one vague complaint about resistance to change.

“Who holds it?” can have more than one answer. A funder, a service operator
and a rule maker can each hold a different part. Write those parts separately.
Give each proposed change an owner, a dependency and an observation that would
show whether it happened.

## 🔄 Follow the conditions recursively

Consider the captured example of free AI compute. The consumer's price
condition leads to the company's question: under what conditions could it
offer that service and keep offering it?

**That question takes the frame down another level.** Each answer can contain
its own if. The point is to expose those dependencies, rather than stop at
the first appealing promise. “Free” describes the consumer's charge. The
conditions for sustaining the offering still need explaining.

Keep discretion under availability. Someone continuing to choose to provide
a service is a different condition from having a durable route to it. The
frame leaves the remedy open. It asks which condition a proposed arrangement
would change, which it would preserve and which it might introduce.

Public, social-insurance and private delivery arrangements can be examined
through the same questions. Their funding and accountability differ. The
starting point remains the person, the service and the conditions of access.

## ⏱ WHEN follows conditions

**WHEN is a prompt for conditional work.** It is not a date, forecast,
guarantee or commitment. The wording is not a validated linguistic test of
someone's role or authority. A person can use the sentence while still
depending on decisions held elsewhere.

Conditions can move concurrently, remain dependency-bound or change in an
unexpected order. A provider may work on one condition while another becomes
binding. The useful update says what changed and how it affects the original
promise. A changed number, a revised rule and a different population are
different updates.

The programme implementation records those differences as **Definition**,
**Evidence**, **Path**, **Actor and authority**, and **Values and loss rule**.
That five-part record is Ren's scaffolding for the captured method, explained
in the [programme](../meta/abundance-transition-programme.md).

> **What changed:** the specific condition, rule or observation.
> **What did not change:** the outcome and parts of the promise still held fixed.
> **What remains blocked:** the dependency still preventing the proposed route.
> **Next observation:** what would change the reading, and when it can be checked.

## 👉 Try both sides

Write the promise from the person's side. Then write the offering side. Name
the conditions on each and connect the ones that belong together. Follow a
dependency until the next conversation has a clear subject and participant.

**Pair that with Goal / Signals / Actions.** Agency is the goal. Conditions
supply the signals. Each action is a hypothesis about moving one of them.
That is the contribution of the frame and method. The choice of a solution
remains open to evidence and argument.

The next useful question is close at hand: which condition is binding today,
who holds it, and what would have to change?
