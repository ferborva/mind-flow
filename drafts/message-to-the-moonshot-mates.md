> **Subject:** A frame for the abundance conversation
>
> Hi Moonshots crew,
>
> I've been giving the abundance conversation a lot of thought. I wanted to
> share a frame that connects the promise with what people see and experience
> day to day.
>
> **You will be able to do X, if...** If you can pay. If it can reach you. If
> the route is permitted. If you can use it.
>
> The piece takes that sentence through a US healthcare example and offers
> five categories to start with. The invitation is to name the conditions,
> ask which one is binding today and identify who holds it.
>
> Then each new announcement has somewhere to land. Which condition changed,
> and for whom?
>
> Piece here: [publication link pending]. I'd love to hear how you would run
> it on the promises you're discussing.
