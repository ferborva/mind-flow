# posts

Finished pieces.

A post got here by passing a provenance pass and a voice pass. It is done.

Sizes, roughly:

- **short**: 300–800 words. One idea, one story, land it.
- **medium**: 800–2000 words. An idea with structure: the claim, why the
  obvious alternative fails, what it looks like in practice.
- **long**: beyond that. Rare as a standalone; usually a long piece wants to
  be a chapter in `books/` instead.

Posts are stable. They get revised when Fer asks, or when a later conversation
genuinely contradicts one, and in that case the revision is recorded in the
commit message and the old claim is noted rather than silently erased.
