# Round 03 deep plan

> **Proposal status:** This is Ren's working plan after Fernando commissioned a
> deep evolution of the abundance transition project. It sharpens Fernando's
> `verb + fill in the blank + if` insight. The sequencing, hypotheses and
> proposed institutions remain agent proposals until Fernando adopts them.

## 🦅 TL;DR

**The goal is not to build an oracle. It is to help named people and legitimate
institutions see when technological capability is, or is not, becoming shared
and durable agency, then prepare and act early without manufacturing certainty,
authority or consent.**

The Observatory is one hypothesis about how to serve that goal. It must compete
against a simpler statistical release and may lose.

Round 03 proceeds through twelve dependency-ordered phases:

```text
mission and value boundaries
→ claim and sentence taxonomy
→ open-world IF semantics
→ evidence and authority kernels
→ affected-party construct governance
→ estimands and signal contracts
→ bounded baseline
→ possible-path and decision-problem definitions
→ preparations and action feasibility
→ forecast targets and shadow scoring
→ public narrative and product trials
→ frozen independent review
```

Every material change follows one challenge cycle:

```text
claim or control
→ strongest cheap counterexample
→ failing test or falsification record
→ smallest correction
→ neighbouring counterexample
→ clean reproduction
→ independent disposition
```

No amount of agent agreement can close legitimacy, authority, affected-party,
legal or operational findings.

## 🎯 The goal and the anti-goals

> **Working goal:** Help named people and legitimate institutions see when
> technological capability is, or is not, becoming shared, durable agency,
> then prepare and act early without manufacturing certainty, authority or
> consent.

This goal has three tests:

1. **Epistemic:** Can people see what is observed, inferred, forecast, contested
   and unknown?
2. **Decision:** Can a named actor identify a legitimate, reversible next step
   early enough to matter?
3. **Human:** Do affected people gain practical choice, security, voice and
   remedy rather than merely becoming easier to measure?

The project is not:

- a claim that abundance is inevitable;
- a single transition score;
- a machine for declaring crises;
- a central planning authority;
- a substitute for democratic negotiation;
- a system for optimising people;
- a persuasive dashboard seeking compliance; or
- evidence that an action is legitimate merely because its conditions passed.

**The Observatory itself is a falsifiable instrument hypothesis:** compared
with a conventional statistical release, does it improve understanding and
decision quality without unacceptable anxiety, stigma, manipulation or false
certainty?

## 🔥 The claim firewall

Before building more signals, split every consequential sentence into atomic
claims. One sentence may contain several claims with different evidence and
publication limits. Each atomic claim receives four independent axes:
**epistemic class**, **provenance role**, **support state** and **publication
disposition**. No axis may stand in for another.

| Axis | Question | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Epistemic class | What kind of assertion is this? | value choice, observation, modelled estimate, derived claim, hypothesis, forecast, scenario, option, commitment, unknown |
| Provenance role | Who or what produced the content? | publisher, affected participant, analyst, model, authorised institution, agent proposal |
| Support state | What does the registered evidence currently do? | supported, contradicted, mixed, insufficient, not tested, expired |
| Publication disposition | Where may it appear now? | prohibited, internal only, methods room, public with label, approved commitment |

Machine validity means that these fields are present and internally
consistent. It does not mean that a claim is true, legitimate or publishable.
The executable prototypes are the
[atomic claim firewall](../evidence/claims/README.md) and
[assumption registry](../evidence/assumptions/README.md). Both remain internal
proposals pending independent and affected-party review.

| Sentence type | What it can say | What defeats or changes it |
|---|---|---|
| Value choice | What future is worth pursuing | Legitimate disagreement changes governance, not a p-value |
| Mission | What the programme tries to enable | Owner review, expiry or changed purpose |
| Observation | What a source reports for a bounded scope | Source correction, extraction failure or incompatible definition |
| Derived claim | What registered arithmetic produces | Reproduction failure, unit mismatch or changed inputs |
| Hypothesis | A testable relationship or mechanism | Registered falsifier or stronger competing account |
| Forecast | A resolvable event with probability or distribution | Resolution and proper scoring |
| Scenario | What follows from explicit assumptions | Changed assumptions, not a surprise observation |
| Option | What a named actor could consider | Gate, safeguard, cost, feasibility or authority failure |
| Commitment | What an authorised actor promises | Expiry, revocation, breach or supersession |
| Unknown | What the evidence cannot yet establish | A governed learning event |

### Current high-risk sentences

| Sentence | Classification now |
|---|---|
| Shared durable agency is a worthwhile destination | Value choice |
| Capability does not guarantee access | Conceptual framing |
| Every useful promise contains relevant conditions | Encoding hypothesis |
| Money is the dominant condition | Scoped research hypothesis |
| Permission becomes dominant next | Scenario or forecast candidate |
| AI is displacing Australian clerical workers | Unsupported now |
| The World is not in an Engels' Pause | Prohibited pending comparable reconstruction |
| NERO reports a modelled employment estimate for a named occupation and region | Modelled estimate, not an observation of employment or causation |
| The Observatory can predict crises early | Instrument hypothesis |
| Prepared actions reduce harm | Intervention hypothesis |
| A named institution will provide support | Commitment only after verified authority |
| Abundance is coming | Not a supported public claim |

**A hypothesis cannot escape defeat by gaining a convenient new IF after its
result is known.** Conditions, loss functions, alternatives and expiry are
registered before evaluation.

## 🧩 Phase 1: Evolve `verb + blank + IF`

Keep the public mnemonic. Compile it into sentence types that separate the
person experiencing an outcome from the actor making a decision.

### Outcome claim

> `[PEOPLE] can [VERB] [OBJECT] at [STANDARD], in [PLACE], during [PERIOD], if
> [REGISTERED CONDITIONS].`

### Forecast

> `There is [PROBABILITY] that [RESOLVABLE EVENT] occurs by [DATE], conditional
> on [ASSUMPTIONS AND INTERVENTION STATE].`

### Option

> `[ACTOR] could consider [VERB] [BOUNDED OBJECT] if [DECISION CONDITIONS],
> blocked if [VETOES].`

### Commitment

> `[AUTHORISED ACTOR] commits to [DELIVERABLE] if [APPROVED TRIGGER], until
> [EXPIRY], with [HELP, APPEAL AND REMEDY].`

### Negotiation

> `[PARTY A] offers [COMMITMENT] if [PARTY B] provides [RECIPROCAL COMMITMENT],
> subject to [HARD SAFEGUARDS, VERIFICATION AND EXIT].`

Every condition edge declares whether it is:

- necessary;
- one sufficient route among alternatives;
- enabling;
- inhibiting;
- correlated only; or
- a decision rule rather than a causal relationship.

Every edge also binds scope, evidence grade, counterevidence, alternatives and
expiry. Logic is open-world: passing every registered condition means only that
the registered route passed. It cannot prove that no omitted condition exists.

Condition evolution is itself a first-class record, not an edit to current
state. Maintain an **append-only condition history** whose typed events are
`added`, `narrowed`, `split`, `merged`, `challenged`, `satisfied`, `failed`,
`expired`, `superseded`, `disputed` and `withdrawn`. Every event binds the
previous wording, previous scope, previous evidence, author, authority where
relevant, reason, effective time, challenges and exact replacement records.
The current state is a reproducible fold over that history. A dashboard crop,
negotiation brief or decision packet must expose both current state and history,
or declare the history omitted and bind the complete ledger.

The executable prototype is the
[condition evolution ledger](../contracts/evolution/README.md). It validates
history structure and integrity only. It does not assess whether a condition is
true or authorise an action.

**Exit:** two independent encoders classify ten real examples consistently.
Hidden-subject, authority-switch, omitted-scope and necessary-versus-sufficient
attacks fail. Undeclared condition rewrites and broken history folds fail.
`UNLESS` never enters stored executable logic.

## 🔐 Phase 2: Evidence and authority kernels

### Evidence kernel

- Content-addressed source bytes and dependency graph.
- Source request, response, publisher vintage, release, retrieval, acquisition,
  computation and revision clocks.
- Point-specific epistemic class and timing assessment.
- Estimand, unit, denominator, population, panel and geography contracts.
- Uncertainty, missingness, revision and correction contracts.
- Logical and physical hashes for data, transformations and public claims.
- Claim ledger v2 with expiry and falsifier.

### Authority kernel

- Repository outputs remain proposals or structural eligibility.
- External identity, signature, scope, expiry and revocation envelope.
- Separate authority, consent, funding, capacity, help and appeal attestations.
- No repository-local fixture can create an operational commitment.
- A separately governed trust registry assigns producer, verifier, reviewer and
  publisher roles. The same identity cannot self-issue evidence, verify it and
  grant publication or operational authority.

**Exit:** every known malicious evidence fixture fails. A signed result cannot
silently change its source, method, claim text or authority. Restricted data use
has an enclave or independent-audit contract rather than a false promise of raw
publication.

## 🫂 Phase 3: Affected-party construct governance

Do not finalise agency, access floors, harms or thresholds before the people
affected can challenge them.

The bounded Australian study needs workers from each scoped occupation, worker
representatives, service and community organisations, disability and language
access perspectives, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander governance suited
to the data and place, statisticians, privacy specialists and delivery experts.

They must be able to contest:

- what agency means in lived terms;
- which outcomes must remain continuous;
- which harms cannot be offset;
- which thresholds are useful or dangerous;
- which data uses and public labels are unacceptable;
- who has decision rights;
- who is absent or unable to participate; and
- what dissent remains unresolved.

The prior recruitment, ethics, dissent and release gates remain frozen in the
[Round 02 affected-party protocol](../reviews/public-comprehension-affected-party-protocol-round-04.md).
The [Round 03 amendments](../reviews/round-03-affected-party-amendments.md)
clarify that numeric coverage floors do not substitute for a prospective power
or precision calculation.

**Exit:** constructs and no-offset harms have named decision ownership. Every
review has a typed governance disposition containing mandate, represented and
missing groups, method, evidence considered, agreement, dissent, conditions,
expiry, withdrawal, compensation and appeal. A boolean acceptance cannot clear
a gate. Ethics, privacy, accessibility and Indigenous governance permit only a
bounded study. Participation by one group never authorises another.

## 📐 Phase 4: Estimands and signal architecture

The executable prototype is the
[signal portfolio contract](../signals/README.md). Its Australian fixture is a
methods-only feasibility candidate with unacquired source bytes and an
externally unverified condition binding.

Every signal contract includes:

```text
construct and estimand
source-native population, place and period
unit and denominator
epistemic class
condition edge and role
leading, confirming, counter, outcome, readiness or harm role
cadence and publication lag
uncertainty and revision behaviour
missingness and exclusions
confounders and negative controls
gaming and manipulation risk
decision lead time
value of additional information
public claim ceiling
```

For each important condition, seek a portfolio: leading signal, confirming
signal, counter-signal, lived outcome, readiness signal, intervention-exposure
signal and information-harm signal. Never compress them into one score.

Signal families cover capability and safety, adoption and work redesign, worker
flows and autonomy, household resources and effective service access,
distribution and severe harm, ownership and bargaining power, ecological
lifecycle burden, institutional delivery and remedy, trust and warning response,
plus positive agency outcomes such as time, health, learning, creativity and
credible choice.

**Exit:** no dashboard join can change scope, population, period or causal role.
Missingness remains visible. Positive and adverse outcomes receive equal
evidential discipline.

## 🇦🇺 Phase 5: Bounded Australian baseline

The current clerical NERO slice is a **feasibility candidate**, not yet an
approved study, causal baseline or warning system. Build candidate graphs
without claiming that any one exists. The technology-push graph is only one:

```text
task exposure
→ actual adoption
→ work redesign
→ worker flow
→ household resources
→ effective service access
→ experienced agency
```

Register at least these competing causal graphs before selecting variables:

1. **Technology-push:** adoption changes work design, flows and household outcomes.
2. **Macro-demand:** demand, interest rates, inflation or public spending move
   employment and adoption together.
3. **Sector-composition:** industry and regional composition change the apparent
   occupation path without a within-workplace technology effect.
4. **Measurement-and-reclassification:** revisions, occupation coding, model
   smoothing or boundary changes create the observed movement.
5. **Policy-and-ownership:** procurement, bargaining, market structure,
   regulation and firm choices determine both deployment and distribution.

No graph is the default causal explanation. Each receives observable
implications, negative controls, timing tests, evidence that would favour a
rival, and a public claim ceiling. A sixth `no material change` graph remains
available when observed differences fit noise, revision or practical
equivalence. The current official-source feasibility limits are recorded in the
[Australia pilot source triage](../research/2026-09-08-australia-pilot-source-triage.md).

Test at least one credible alternative at every arrow. Keep occupations and
regions separate. Map suppression, classification drift and incompatible
geographies. Compare metrics with worker accounts and frontline service
evidence. Define the ecological lifecycle boundary.

**Exit:** workers recognise the measured gains and losses as relevant.
Aggregate place evidence never substitutes for cohort evidence. Exposure never
becomes adoption. NERO never corroborates its own Labour Force inputs. No
causal, crisis or national claim is emitted.

## 🛤️ Phase 6: Possible paths as hypotheses

The executable prototype is the
[possible-path contract](../paths/README.md). It treats a machine-valid path as
an unscored hypothesis and grants no truth, probability, crisis or action
authority.

A possible path stores:

```text
scoped favourable or adverse outcome
initiating conditions
mechanism edges and evidence grade
strongest competing path
leading, confirming and counter-signals
resolvable event definition and base rate
action lead time and intervention state
forecast eligibility
affected and omitted populations
gaming and reflexivity
expiry and abandonment rule
```

Test favourable and adverse paths. Do not force symmetry when evidence differs.
Candidate paths include capability without safe reach, adoption bottlenecks,
local transition breaches, household continuity breaches, essential-work
squeezes, concentration, ecological capacity, governance overload and harmful
warning effects. Favourable candidates include effective access, earnings or
time gains, stronger continuity floors, greater contestability, lower lifecycle
burden per outcome and greater refusal, switching and remedy.

**Exit:** every crisis candidate is a resolvable scoped event rather than a
story. Unscored paths render as hypotheses, never coloured probabilities. The
word `crisis` remains blocked until its definition passes review.

## 🧰 Phase 7: Design preparation before forecasting

The executable prototype is the
[early-action preparation contract](../preparation/README.md). Its four-scale
fixture is synthetic and grants no authority.

Forecast only events for which earlier knowledge could alter a legitimate
decision. Each candidate action records the protected outcome, alternatives,
owner, authority, preparation and lifecycle gates, cost, opportunity cost,
minimum useful lead time, capacity, consent, privacy, appeal, distribution,
dependencies, compensation and sunset.

Preparation classes:

1. **Observation capacity:** data, vintages, challenge and correction.
2. **Low-regret capacity:** registries, payment rails, appeals, staffing and
   interoperable records.
3. **Negotiated readiness:** reserves, procurement, worker agreements and
   service capacity.
4. **Conditional delivery:** reversible support after an authorised trigger.
5. **Fresh authority required:** coercion, surveillance, rights restriction or
   irreversible allocation.

“Low regret” is itself a hypothesis. Costs and foregone alternatives count.
Readiness evidence does not establish intervention effectiveness. It measures
whether a nominated capability exists under test conditions, not whether using
it improves the protected outcome.

Preparation must be reversible by default. Irreversible preparation crosses a
higher gate with fresh authority, proportionality, rights review and a stronger
evidence standard. A narrow containment exception may permit temporary,
reviewable protection where delay risks imminent serious harm, but it cannot
silently become the material intervention.

A fail-closed result does not establish that inaction is safe. It only blocks
an unverified machine action. The record must name the risk of delay, the
human escalation route, any bounded precaution available, and who owns the
decision not to act.

**Exit:** every forecast candidate has a real decision, owner and minimum lead
time. Individual preparation never privatises structural failure. A country
plan never invents powers. No option renders as an instruction.

## 🔭 Phase 8: Forecast and crisis-point validation

The prospective machinery is maintained in the
[forecast registry and scoring contract](../forecasts/README.md).

Use a maturity ladder:

1. Scenario without probability.
2. Source-value shadow forecast that tests plumbing only.
3. Scoped outcome forecast with a declared baseline.
4. Threshold-event forecast with an action lead time.
5. Decision-useful forecast after enough prospective scoring.
6. Conditional operational forecast accounting for interventions and warning
   exposure.

Every forecast binds target, resolution source, cohort, place, horizon,
issue-time cutoff, probability or distribution, naive and reference-class
baselines, uncertainty, model disagreement, intervention assumptions, warning
exposure, scores, stop rules and void conditions.

Operational maturity and inferential target are separate axes. A research-only
forecast may have no operational owner, but it cannot render as a warning,
trigger or decision aid. A decision-linked forecast needs a named owner,
minimum useful lead time, intended decision, loss function and authority path.
Prospective holdout evidence is required before claiming predictive skill;
historical plumbing checks and one resolved case do not qualify.

**Exit:** prospective forecasts beat declared baselines on frozen holdout
evidence. Calibration, discrimination and decision utility remain separate.
Failure narrows or retires the forecast class.

## 🌌 Phase 9: Public narrative and Observatory experience

The public story must not make one political future feel empirically ordained.
It presents at least four legitimate horizon stances with equal epistemic
discipline: capability **expansion**, access and ownership **redistribution**,
resource or deployment **reduction**, and **no-deployment or refusal** where
people reject the system or its terms.

The public story is:

1. **Possibility:** technology may expand what people can do.
2. **Condition:** possibility becomes lived agency only through named IFs.
3. **Reality:** here is what is observed, unknown and contested now.
4. **Paths:** here are favourable and adverse hypotheses and their
   discriminators.
5. **Agency:** here are personal options, collective choices, institutional
   duties, commitments and preparation gaps.
6. **Memory:** here are forecasts, corrections, dissent and prior performance.
7. **Horizon:** here is what greater agency would look like in time, health,
   learning, security, creativity, connection and credible choice.

The opening layer answers only:

```text
WHAT CHANGED
WHO THIS DOES AND DOES NOT DESCRIBE
WHAT IT MEANS AND DOES NOT MEAN
ACTION OR NO ACTION
NEXT CHECK OR CHALLENGE
```

The seven-part update is the second layer. Methods, bytes, path graphs,
forecasts, options, dissent and corrections are the third.

Hari Seldon influences mathematical memory, branching paths and visible model
revision. He does not supply an oracle voice.

**Exit:** a fictional-data prototype passes accessibility and dangerous-
inference tests. Evidence-bearing screens pass source-to-pixel tests. Audience
adaptation never changes facts, uncertainty, forecast or authority state.

## 🧪 Phase 10: Test the instrument itself

The proposed public entry point is
[The Future Has Conditions](../communications/transition-field-guide.md). It
turns the IF grammar, condition history, seven signal roles and preparation
ladder into a reader-facing field guide. Its claims remain bounded by the
underlying contracts, and its framing must be tested rather than assumed clear.

The proposed design is maintained in the
[Observatory public-instrument comparison](../experiments/observatory-comparison/README.md).

Compare three arms:

1. conventional statistical release;
2. release plus facilitated deliberation; and
3. Observatory plus identical deliberation.

Measure scope and uncertainty comprehension, observation versus inference,
forecast versus scenario, option versus commitment, evidence-and-authority-
appropriate action selection and appeal,
false certainty, false reassurance, anxiety, stigma, challenge behaviour,
decision quality, time and subgroup harms.

**Exit:** no dangerous-understanding gate fails. Directly affected groups are
not averaged away. The Observatory adds value over the simpler baseline. If it
does not, narrow the product to provenance, forecast memory or professional
evidence infrastructure.

## 🚨 Phase 11: No-consequence rehearsal

Run shadow and tabletop cycles for warning misses, false alarms, delayed data,
source outages, authority expiry, capacity failure, appeal backlog, locally
hidden harm, interventions changing forecasts, gaming, anxiety, stigma, pause,
reversal, recovery and compensation.

**Exit:** delivery, help and appeal meet registered service levels. Recovery
survives simultaneous pause or reversal. Failure cannot silently graduate or
withdraw support. Public consequence remains blocked until human and
institutional gates pass.

## 🥊 Assumptions we will keep attacking

- More options necessarily increase agency.
- A shared definition of abundance is possible or desirable.
- Conditions can be observed before harm becomes irreversible.
- The registered IF graph is complete enough to guide action.
- Thresholds transport across people, places or political systems.
- Early preparation is low regret.
- A warning does not help cause the event.
- Better information produces better decisions.
- People trust, understand or can access a dashboard.
- Metrics cannot be gamed or captured.
- National aggregates describe lived experience.
- Representatives can legitimately speak for everyone affected.
- Technology is an external wave rather than a result of policy and ownership.
- Forecast relationships survive institutional response.
- Federation preserves sovereignty rather than creating central authority.
- Positive-horizon language does not pressure people to endorse a transition.

Every item becomes one of four things: a bounded hypothesis, a hard safeguard,
a legitimate value decision or an explicit unknown. None stays invisible.
