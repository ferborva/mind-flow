# Themes

Themes are not planned. They are noticed.

When the same idea surfaces in a third unrelated conversation, that is a theme,
and it gets a slug here. Themes tag seeds, drafts and posts, and they are what
eventually cluster into books.

A theme is only real once three separate captures touch it. Two is a
coincidence; anything less is a guess about what he cares about, which is not
mine to make.
