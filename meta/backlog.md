# Backlog

Questions for Fer. This is how the repository pulls material out of him instead
of waiting.

Every gap found while writing lands here rather than being filled with a guess.
When he has a random moment and asks "what should we talk about?", this file is
the answer.

Keep it short and specific. "Tell me about leadership" is useless. "You said
the second rewrite was the one that worked — what changed between the first and
the second?" gets a real answer.

## Open

- [ ] **The three foundation documents.** Constitution, communication style,
      soul representation. Nothing can be written in his voice until these are
      in `foundation/`. Highest priority by a distance.
- [ ] **Scope of "work experience."** Which years, which roles, which domains?
      Not to constrain it — to know when a conversation has wandered somewhere
      new and worth marking.
- [ ] **Audience.** Who is the reader? His future self, people he has managed,
      strangers on the internet, his kids? This changes how much context every
      piece needs to carry.
- [ ] **Does any of this leave the repository?** Blog, newsletter, published
      book — or is it private for now? Affects nothing about the writing, but
      affects whether there is a publishing gate to build.
- [ ] **Cadence.** How often should scheduled runs fire? Daily is likely too
      eager for a repo fed by occasional conversations; weekly may be right.
