# Backlog

Questions for Fer. This is how the repository pulls material out of him instead
of waiting.

Every gap found while writing lands here rather than being filled with a guess.
When he has a random moment and asks "what should we talk about?", this file is
the answer.

Keep it short and specific. "Tell me about leadership" is useless. "You said
the second rewrite was the one that worked. What changed between the first and
the second?" gets a real answer.

One question per turn when actually asking him. This list is the queue, not the
script.
