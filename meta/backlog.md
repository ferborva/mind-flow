# Backlog

Questions for Fer. This is how the repository pulls material out of him instead
of waiting.

Every gap found while writing lands here rather than being filled with a guess.
When he has a random moment and asks "what should we talk about?", this file is
the answer.

Keep it short and specific. "Tell me about leadership" is useless. "You said
the second rewrite was the one that worked. What changed between the first and
the second?" gets a real answer.

One question per turn when actually asking him. This list is the queue, not the
script.

      repo will rarely have. Worth a sentence from him to confirm.

      idea, and naming is the one checklist item I can supply without breaching

### Scope and shape

- [ ] **Scope of "work experience."** The style profile gives the current role:
      Senior Engineering Manager at Atlassian, Ecosystem / Forge Storage. What
      came before? Not to constrain the material, but so I can tell when a
      conversation has wandered into new territory worth marking.
- [ ] **Audience.** Who is the reader? His future self, engineers he has
      managed, other engineering managers, strangers, his kids? This changes how
      much context every piece has to carry.
- [ ] **Does any of this leave the repository?** Blog, newsletter, a real book,
      or private for now? `soul.md` already settles the mechanics (anything with
      external side effects gets drafted and approved first), so this is about
      intent, not permission.
- [ ] **Cadence.** How often should scheduled runs fire? Daily is too eager for
      a repo fed by occasional conversations. Weekly is my recommendation.
