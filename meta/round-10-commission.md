2. Fernando's storm definition, and the assumptions this round runs under
His words, capture/2026-09-10-storms-as-social-contract-shifts.md:

a storm can be considered as a point of major shift in the social contract that can lead to a large enough disturbance of social fabric. let's say a change that can affect 5% of society in a country or more. Each country could/would have their own "storm"/crisis moments in isolation or there could be a broader one hitting globally or semi-globally at the same time.

I would simplify it by access to means to generate income as that is a fundamental key to access many others and be part of the social fabric

Three questions he has not answered. This round proceeds under stated assumptions, every one of them marked provenance: commissioned-proposal, reversible, and listed in meta/backlog.md as his to overturn:

Which shifts. His clarification answers this at the level of principle: disruption of access to means of generating income. Assumption: the indicator families in Workstream B are the operational reading of that principle, offered for him to strike or add.
The "naughty kid" line stays as an observation and carries no remedy. He settled this on 2026-09-10: "indeed that is just a plain observation at this point of where the money accrues to". It now lives in drafts/name-the-if.md after the 2026-09-11 consolidation.

One more of his views binds the design: on 2026-09-08 he conceded that the order in which conditions fall cannot be planned. So a storm criterion diagnoses that a shift is under way or has happened and names which condition moved. It does not schedule. Forecasts remain separate, preregistered, single-cell, and scored in public.

Workstream B: Operationalise the storm criterion at country level
The criterion, as a commissioned proposal in signals/countries/storm-criterion.v1.md:

A candidate storm in country C over period P is a measured change in the share of C's population whose access to means of generating income was disrupted, of at least 5 percentage points of population, or a measured change in which condition category binds that access. Regional and global candidates are the co-occurrence of national candidates, never the sum of their affected shares.

Indicator families, each an operational reading of "access to means of generating income", each needing retained bytes for at least 40 of 50 countries from one vintage, licence status per source, and a stated relationship to one of his five categories. Candidates to verify, not assume: ILO unemployment and labour underutilisation (modelled and reported, kept distinct); ILO employment-to-population ratio; ILO informal employment share; ILO or OECD real wage index; World Bank poverty headcount at the retained thresholds (nowcast versus survey vintages kept distinct); IMF WEO employment and output-gap series; national food-CPI components where an international publisher aggregates them. For each: what "disrupted" means in that series, what 5 percentage points of population corresponds to, and what the series cannot see (informal work, hours, quality, household dependants).
Retain and measure. Same discipline as Round 09: bytes, headers, hashes, selectors, footnotes, reproducible derivation, evidence ceiling. Producers replayed in CI from day one, not added at seal.
Retrospective check of the criterion, not a calibration claim. For each retained series with history, compute where the criterion would have fired in the last 20 years. Compare against a short retained list of widely recorded shocks (2008 to 2009, 2020) as a sanity check of the criterion's behaviour. State plainly that this is a check of the criterion's mechanics on known history, that it establishes no forecast skill, and that a criterion which fires everywhere or nowhere is wrong. Write up misses and false fires as the deliverable.
Per-country diagnosis. For each country and period: no candidate, candidate (which series, which category moved, both affected-share readings), or cannot say (which series is missing). The Round 09 reader takes these as new rows. Nothing says "storm detected" in headline text; the state is "candidate", and the page says what would make it more or less than that.
IF evolution. Each new indicator family becomes a condition definition with condition_category, bound to the storm criterion as its consumer. Every source discovery that changes a construct becomes a new identity under the Round 09.1 policy, with the old consumer binding invalidated and rebound, and a test that fails on the old hash. Report the count truthfully.
