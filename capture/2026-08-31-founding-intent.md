# What this repository is for

**Context.** The first conversation. Fer opened an empty repository and
described what he wanted it to become. Captured from his own words in that
message — the founding statement of intent.

---

## The intention

> I want to initiate this repository with the intention of it being the house
> for me to slowly and gradually build all the knowledge I have built over the
> years in my work experience.

Two words doing a lot of work: *slowly* and *gradually*. This is explicitly not
a project with a deadline or a finished shape in mind.

## How the writing happens

> I would like it to be mostly if not fully written by you as post-conversational
> encounters that you and I will have when I have a random moment. I would talk
> with you and you would then document everything I say, close to word for word,
> and then through scheduled run gradually build, edit, review, publish, …
> content.

"When I have a random moment" is the constraint the whole system is built
around. The conversations are unscheduled and short; the work of turning them
into writing is scheduled and happens without him.

"Close to word for word" — his instruction on capture fidelity, stated before
anything was built.

## Where it goes

> You'd gradually build small post style writings, medium sized, and as the
> collection grows would build a book or several of them hehehe let's see how we
> go.

<!-- NOTE: "hehehe let's see how we go" — the books are a real ambition held
     lightly. Worth remembering when the pipeline is tempted to declare a book
     too early. The lightness is the instruction. -->

## The founding documents

> I will give you to kick off the repository my personal constitution, my
> communication style document and soul representation.

These were promised in this conversation but not yet provided.

---

## Loose ends

- The three foundation documents have not arrived yet.
- No sense yet of which years or roles the "work experience" covers.
- No sense yet of who the reader is.
- "Publish" was used but not defined — inside the repo, or out to the world?
