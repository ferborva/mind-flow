# A softer view on tax and the portfolio remedy

**Context.** During an independent review of PR #2, the reviewer flagged that
`drafts/abundance-has-an-if.md` had been rewritten to soften his 2026-09-01
position on tax and UBI and to propose a "portfolio" remedy, with no capture
supporting the change. The reviewer laid out which passages the 2026-09-08
conversation supported and which it did not, and asked him directly whether he
now holds the softened tax view and the portfolio remedy, or whether his
position is still that the money accrues to companies and the choice is theirs.

**Why this is a capture.** It is a provenance event. It resolves an open
contradiction between the 2026-09-01 capture, the untouched seed
`the-choice-belongs-to-enterprises`, and the rewritten draft.

---

## His answer, verbatim

> I hold a much softer view on the tax view and portfolio remedy. My take is best
> placed at the reframe and method level vs the solutioning

---

## What this settles

- **Tax.** The 2026-09-01 line "I don't think tax is a reasonable way" is no
  longer his firm position. He now holds "a much softer view". He did not state
  a replacement position on tax.
- **The portfolio remedy.** He is also softer on the portfolio remedy. He did
  not adopt the specific portfolio wording (prices, earnings, ownership,
  transfers, public provision, institutions). That wording remains Ren's.
- **Where his contribution sits.** His take belongs "at the reframe and method
  level vs the solutioning". Any draft in his voice should present the frame
  and the method, and hold the remedy open, rather than asserting a specific
  solution as his.

## Follow-up, verbatim

Asked whether he still prefers the visible-price model and whether displaced
workers should still be first across the "enough" line:

> visible-price model is a solution. And the "enough" line is too blurry to lean
> on active for now. Driving through the "IFs" framework would simplify things.
> That put side by side the Goal Signals Actions framework as an overaching way
> to discuss the action plans further down and what to monitor should help

## What the follow-up settles

- **Visible-price (Belgian) model.** Classified by him as "a solution", so it
  falls on the solutioning side and is no longer a position to carry in his
  voice. The Belgium/Spain contrast can survive as an illustration of a
  condition, not as a preference.
- **The "enough to make working optional" line.** "Too blurry to lean on active
  for now." Not withdrawn, but parked. Drafts should not build an argument on
  it, including the "displaced workers first" priority that depended on it.
- **Method.** The IFs framework is the driving instrument. Alongside it, the
  Goal / Signals / Actions framework (he says "Goal Signals Actions", the
  Google GSM structure he raised on 2026-09-08) is the overarching way to
  discuss action plans and what to monitor. This confirms and extends seed
  `agency-is-the-goal-conditions-are-the-signals`.

## What this does not settle

- Whether "the choice is the companies' to make" survives in any form. Given
  the above it reads as solutioning too, but he has not said so.
- What his softer view on tax actually is. Only that it is softer.
