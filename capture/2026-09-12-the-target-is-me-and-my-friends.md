# The target is me and my friends

**Context.** Round 11 stopped because the agents could not proceed without two
decisions only Fer can make. Their study-readiness review lists eleven blockers;
nine of them are unanswerable until someone names who the thing is for and who
is responsible for it. I put both questions to him plainly. He answered both.

**Why this is a capture.** Two provenance events. The first sets the audience
for the Observatory and the decision experience. The second is Fer accepting
named accountability, which is the gate every downstream approval hangs on.

---

## His answer on who it is for, verbatim

> Me and my friends. We will be the de target.

### What this settles

- **The audience is him and his friends.** Not recruited strangers, not a
  general public, not a workplace cohort.
- This is the same circle as the Moonshot crew framing he gave on 2026-09-07
  ("the Moonshot crew, argument-first"), but stated in his own wider terms.
  Whether "my friends" and "the Moonshot crew" are the same set is his to say.
- **It is still people.** A friendly audience lowers the recruitment and
  compensation questions. It does not remove consent, withdrawal, or the
  accessibility question about whether the thing is readable at all.

### What this does not settle

- Who the friends are, how many, and whether he intends to name them.
- Whether they are being shown the thing to give an opinion, or being measured
  on whether it changed their understanding. Those are different exercises with
  different obligations.
- Whether the study framing survives at all at this size. Eleven blockers were
  scoped for a recruited sample; most of that machinery may be wrong for a
  handful of friends.

## His answer on ownership, verbatim

He selected, from the options put to him:

> You, explicitly

### What this settles

- **Fer is the named accountable lead.** He accepted the option whose stated
  meaning was: "You accept being the named accountable lead. Unblocks them
  immediately, and means anything that goes wrong lands on you."
- The `external-authority-verifier` gate in
  `experiments/decision-experience/readiness-summary.json` is explicit that an
  agent-entered reference never converts into approval. **This capture is the
  record of what he said. It is not itself an approval artefact**, and the
  agents still have to route it through whatever verification they designed.

### What this does not settle

- Whether he also takes the independent-safety-monitor role, which their design
  deliberately separates from the lead. One person holding both defeats the
  point of having two.
- Whether he wants an ethics determination at this size, or considers showing
  something to friends outside that frame.
- Resourcing. He accepted responsibility, not a time commitment.

## Loose ends

  rather than a recruited sample.
- Ask him whether "my friends" and "the Moonshot crew" are the same people.
- Ask him whether this is feedback or measurement. The answer changes most of
  what follows.
- Ask who holds the safety-monitor role, given he now holds the lead.
