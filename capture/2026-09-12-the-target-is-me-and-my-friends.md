# The station is for us, the posts are for the world

**Context.** Round 11 stopped because the agents could not proceed without two
decisions only Fer can make. Their study-readiness review lists eleven blockers;
nine of them are unanswerable until someone names who the thing is for and who
is responsible for it. I put both questions to him plainly, he answered both,
and then answered three follow-ups in the same session.

**Why this is a capture.** Six provenance events in one session: the audience
for the station, Fer accepting named accountability, the exercise being
feedback plus his own judgement of impact, his friends being a different set
from the Moonshot crew, his wife accepting the safety-monitor role, and last
and largest, the scope statement that separates the private tool from the
public writing.

---

## His answer on who it is for, verbatim

> Me and my friends. We will be the de target.

### What this settles

- **The audience is him and his friends.** Not recruited strangers, not a
  general public, not a workplace cohort.
- **Not the Moonshot crew.** He confirmed that later in the same session, below.
  The crew he named on 2026-09-07 ("the Moonshot crew, argument-first") is the
  audience for the writing. His friends are the audience for the station.
- **It is still people.** A friendly audience lowers the recruitment and
  compensation questions. It does not remove consent, withdrawal, or the
  accessibility question about whether the thing is readable at all.

### What this does not settle

- Who the friends are, how many, and whether he intends to name them.
- Whether the study framing survives at all at this size. Eleven blockers were
  scoped for a recruited sample; most of that machinery may be wrong for a
  handful of friends.

Feedback versus measurement was open at this point and he answered it below.

## His answer on ownership, verbatim

He selected, from the options put to him:

> You, explicitly

### What this settles

- **Fer is the named accountable lead.** He accepted the option whose stated
  meaning was: "You accept being the named accountable lead. Unblocks them
  immediately, and means anything that goes wrong lands on you."
- The `external-authority-verifier` gate in
  `experiments/decision-experience/readiness-summary.json` is explicit that an
  agent-entered reference never converts into approval. **This capture is the
  record of what he said. It is not itself an approval artefact**, and the
  agents still have to route it through whatever verification they designed.

### What this does not settle

- Whether he wants an ethics determination at this size, or considers showing
  something to friends outside that frame.
- Resourcing. He accepted responsibility, not a time commitment.

## The three follow-ups, same session

I put the three open questions above back to him. He answered all three.

### On feedback versus measurement, verbatim

> We will give feedback and I will additional gauge the impact of the
> information on their overview of the path forward.

**It is both, and the second half is his own judgement rather than an
instrument.** They give feedback. Separately, he gauges whether the information
shifted how they see the path forward.

What this settles:

- **There is an impact question**, so this is not purely "what do you think of
  the interface". Something is being assessed.
- **He is the one assessing it.** No rubric, no endpoint, no coding scheme, no
  statistics. His read of his friends.
- The thing being gauged is their **overview of the path forward**, not their
  comprehension of a number or their performance on a task.

What this does not settle:

- He is presenter, author and judge in the same exercise. That is a real
  confound and it is his to accept or mitigate. Worth saying once and then
  letting him decide.
- Whether anything is written down. A gauge that leaves no record cannot be
  wrong later, which is also the reason it cannot teach anything later.
- Whether the eleven-blocker apparatus applies at all at this shape. My read:
  most of it does not, and the honest move is to shrink it rather than clear it.

### On whether the friends are the Moonshot crew, verbatim

> No

**Two distinct audiences, confirmed.** The Moonshot crew is the audience for the
writing (`drafts/name-the-if.md`, `drafts/message-to-the-moonshot-mates.md`,
and the observatory framing he set on 2026-09-07). His friends are the audience
for the station. These should stop being conflated in the programme documents.

Not settled: who the friends are, how many, or whether he will name them.

### On who holds the safety-monitor role, verbatim

> My wife

**Recorded as his decision.** Their Round 11 design names this role
`independent-safety-monitor` and asks for someone with actual pause and
escalation authority, deliberately kept separate from the lead.

Stated plainly and once, because it is his call: **a spouse is separate from
him but is not independent in the sense that design means.** At this scale, a
handful of friends giving feedback with no data collection, that may be exactly
proportionate and the formal role may be the wrong frame entirely. Recorded as
what he said, flagged, not argued.

He confirmed below that she is on board. Still not settled: whether she knows
what pause authority would mean in practice.

## The scope statement, same session

Closing the two loose ends above, he answered something larger than either.

### Verbatim

> My gauge will be shared here.

> My wife is on board.

> This tool is first and foremost for us. Not for the world (yet at least) we
> are looking for personal value and ideas.

> To share will be the posts and the reframe

### What this settles

**The station is a private instrument.** It is for him and his friends. It is
not a product, not a public service, and not a thing being validated for
anyone else's use. The purpose he names is **personal value and ideas**.

**What goes to the world is the writing.** The posts and the reframe. Nothing
else leaves.

That is a clean division of labour and it resolves a tension that had been
building in the programme:

| | Audience | Purpose | Leaves the repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| The station | Him and his friends | Personal value and ideas | No |
| The posts and the reframe | The world, via the Moonshot crew | The argument | Yes |

**His gauge lands here.** Whatever he reads off his friends' reaction becomes a
capture in this repository, which means it enters the pipeline as his substance
rather than evaporating.

**His wife has accepted the pause role.** Recorded as stated.

### What follows from it

- **This is not a human study.** Showing a private tool to friends for their
  reactions is not research with participants, and most of the eleven readiness
  blockers were built for something this is not. Recruitment, compensation, an
  ethics determination, allocation and coding, power and worthwhile effect,
  preregistration and freeze: these do not apply to a thing he is not running.
  Re-scope, do not clear. Clearing implies they were satisfied.
- **What survives is small and still worth keeping.** Whether the thing is
  readable and usable, whether his friends know what they are looking at, and
  whether anything he collects about them stays private. That is roughly it.
- **"Yet at least" is doing real work.** He is leaving the door open, not
  closing it. The gates should be re-scoped rather than deleted, so that a
  later decision to go public has something to walk back through.

### What this does not settle

- Whether the reframe goes out as one of the posts or as something separate.
- What form his gauge takes when it is shared here. A capture after each
  session, a running note, or something he writes himself.
- Whether "the posts" means the current drafts specifically or the pipeline in
  general.

## Loose ends

- Separate the two audiences in the programme documents. The Moonshot crew and

- Ask what form his gauge takes when it lands here, and whether he wants a
