# Endorsing the red team, and commissioning a transition design

**Context.** After reading `research/2026-09-07-red-team-moonshot.md`. Two things
happen here, and the first matters more than it looks.

**Why this is a capture and not just an instruction.** He explicitly adopts a
position that was, until now, my construction rather than his. That is a
provenance event. The Blundin transmission argument was written by me as a
steelman; he has now endorsed it, so it becomes citable as his view.

---

## The endorsement

> The counter piece from the moonshot mates is pure gold. I agree with many
> things there and made me reflect on my weakest points.

He does not say which of his points he now considers weak. Worth asking.

## The argument he singles out

> This from DB2 is strong:
>
> If the labour channel is being deliberately automated away, one of the two
> transmission mechanisms is being severed by the same investment. Reinvestment
> is a complete answer only if prices fall faster than wages disappear, which is
> a testable claim and nobody is testing it

<!-- NOTE: he is quoting back the defence I wrote for him against the Blundin
     objection, not the objection itself. So what he is endorsing is his own
     best counter-argument, which he had not previously articulated. From here
     it is his position. -->

The core of it, in his framing: **money reaches people through wages or through
prices. Automating the labour channel severs one of the two, using the same
capital that is supposed to deliver the other. So reinvestment only works if
prices fall faster than wages disappear.** And that is a testable claim that
nobody is testing.

## What he commissioned

> I'd like you to take my piece and combine it with the take from the Mates to
> build a piece that proposes: a multi year transition (consider human change
> management rates ADKAR framework) for how we can reach abundance

With four required contents, his wording:

1. The interactions between governments and productive businesses and how they
   evolve.
2. How the IF conditions of the foundational equation evolve.
3. Which signals can and should be monitored to understand progress.
4. Which critical transitions social movements can be forecasted as hard to
   endure, and how they can be navigated.

Note "the foundational equation". He is now treating the verb + blank + if
structure as an established object with a name, which it was not a week ago.

---

## Loose ends

- **Which of his points does he now consider weak?** He says the red team made
  him reflect on them and does not name them. The single most useful follow-up.
- He asks for a piece that *proposes* a transition. Most of a transition design
  is not in any capture, so it cannot be his substance. Handled by writing it as
  an explicitly commissioned proposal for his review rather than as his settled
  view. He should confirm that framing is what he wanted.
- "Human change management rates" suggests he has a view on how fast populations
  can absorb change. He does not say what it is.
- ADKAR is an individual-level model. He asks for it at civilisational scale. He
  may or may not have noticed the stretch.
