# The weather station watches the world

**Country-level breadth, with the Australian care pathway as worked depth.**
Fernando wants a “weather station for the approaching storms of change”
monitoring broad market shifts across at least the top 50 economies. The GP
example demonstrates the method; it does not define the programme's limits.

## His words

> I want to have this "weather station for the
> approaching storms of change" to be monitoring broad market shifts at a country level
> for several key countries in the world. At least the top 50 countries in the worlds
> economy.
> from: 2026-09-10-where-the-money-sits-and-the-weather-station

His urgency is also captured: “we are in the midst of the singularity” and
“the so called super sonic tsunami will come faster than we think”. These are
his views, not measured results or forecasts issued by the Observatory.

## Related thread: crisis points and an order that cannot be planned

On September 8 he imagined Seldon-style crisis points society could prepare
for as conditions change. Later in that conversation he said:

> I don't think the sequencing is something that can actually be planned for to bring down conditions.
> from: 2026-09-08-abundance-frame-conversation

On September 9 he put the IFs beside Goal / Signals / Actions. Agency is the
goal; conditions provide signals; actions are hypotheses about changing them.
The world-scale scope extends that investigation, not the claim that agency
has become directly measurable.

## What this does not settle

- Which ranking and vintage define the top 50 economies.
- Which broad market shifts matter most, and which series can represent them.
- What counts as a storm rather than ordinary movement.
- How his crisis-point forecasting ambition sits with his sequencing concession.

Diagnose per country, notice change and avoid scheduling the transition is a
commissioned reading to discuss, not a newly captured answer to the last
question. Rankings, signal selections and numerical thresholds remain proposals.
His five categories are the lens: price, permission, proximity, availability
and capability, with discretion distinguished within availability.
