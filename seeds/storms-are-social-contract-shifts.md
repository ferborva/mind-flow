# Storms are major shifts in the social contract

**Fernando offers a working definition through the social contract and social fabric.**
His phrase “can be considered as” leaves the definition provisional, beyond an unusual market movement.

## His words

> a storm can be considered as a point of major shift in the social contract that can lead to a large enough disturbance of social fabric.

Source: [his September 10 answer](../capture/2026-09-10-storms-as-social-contract-shifts.md#his-answer-verbatim).

## What this does not settle

Whether a beneficial shift is a storm, or only a harmful one; how to observe a
social-contract shift; its duration or severity. A market series is not itself
proof of this construct. Operational criteria remain Ren's commissioned work.

The [scale](five-percent-is-the-storm-scale.md),
[income-access focus](income-access-is-the-storm-focus.md) and
[geographic scope](storms-can-be-national-or-shared.md) are separate seeds.
