# The choice belongs to the enterprises, not the governments

> **Superseded 2026-09-09.** This was his position on 2026-09-01. He endorsed the
> red team that broke it (2026-09-07), did not defend it when the 2026-09-08
> conversation called the ending "a moral question, and you got broken on it",
> and on 2026-09-09 said: "I hold a much softer view on the tax view and
> portfolio remedy. My take is best placed at the reframe and method level vs
> the solutioning." The seed is kept because the chain always gets kept. Do not
> write from the position below as his current view. What replaced it as a
> question, not a remedy, lives in `run-the-frame-on-the-supplier` and
> `if-is-demand-when-is-supply`.

Original 2026-09-01 position: if labour is displaced, money still has to reach
people. Tax and UBI are the wrong lever. The money accrues to a handful of
companies, so the decision is theirs, and waiting for government to force it is a
deadlock.

## Stories and evidence

**Belgium vs Spain**, his worked contrast for how money flows back:
- Belgium: you see the full cost, you pay it, social services reimburse you. Net
  zero cost to the user, but the user keeps a reference for what things are worth.
- Spain: you pay nothing at the point of use, funded by tax upstream.
He prefers the visible-cost model for the reference of contrast it preserves.

**His proposal**, said with deliberate looseness: Tesla gyms, SpaceX theatres,
OpenAI movie making, Claude medical services. Free of charge to anyone in the
world. Services brought genuinely to zero cost as the mechanism for
participation, rather than cash transfers.

**The reinvestment counter-argument**, which he raises against himself: companies
will say the profits go back to society as new jobs, new data centres, new power
plants, or as lower prices. He notes the jobs answer holds "at least for now,
and sometimes that might disappear". 

## Caveats and edges

He does not resolve whether a cap on accrual is legitimate. He names it as
opening "the door to socialist discussions", states the counter-position
seriously ("that money is well earned by the founders of a massive business,
it's theirs by right"), and leaves it open.

## What survives, 2026-09-09

- **Tax.** "A much softer view." No replacement position stated.
- **The company remedy.** Withdrawn as a remedy. Whether "the money accrues
  there, so the choice is theirs" survives as a diagnosis is unanswered; GAP
  left in the draft.
- **Belgium vs Spain.** He now classes the visible-price preference as "a
  solution". The contrast survives only as an illustration of one condition
  being met two ways.
- **The naughty-kid line.** Description of where the ball is, not a remedy.
  Kept in the draft on that basis (Ren's reading, not his statement).
