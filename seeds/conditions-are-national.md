# The conditions are national, and countries are not going anywhere

Technology disperses globally. The conditions attached to it do not. Each `if`
stays bound to a country's laws and government, and that stays true until
countries stop existing, which is not happening soon.

## In his words

> If we retain these countries, the countries will continue to impose their own
> ifs, as the technology disperses around the world.
> from: 2026-09-01-abundance-is-conditional

> We don't live in a globalised world under a single nation where humanity is
> understood to be one.
> from: 2026-09-01-abundance-is-conditional

## Stories and evidence

**Electric vehicles.** China has them, America has them, Europe has them, all
under their own ifs. The technology dispersed; the conditions did not converge.
He expects AI to follow the identical path.

**The China framing.** He hears the Moonshot crew say "we have to beat China"
repeatedly, and hears nobody attempting a humanity-level frame: "I'm far from
hearing them uniting forces on a humanity standard, or even trying." 

## Caveats and edges

He raises an exit from the premise and drops it half-formed: an exodus from
Earth, new countries on the moon or Mars, carrying the same if conditions with
them. Kept as said, not developed.

- Is the moon/Mars thread a throwaway or a piece of its own?
- Does he think the competitive frame is wrong, or just insufficient?
