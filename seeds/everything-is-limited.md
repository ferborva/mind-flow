# Everything is limited, so abundance needs a floor not a promise

"You can have anything" is false on physics before it is false on economics.
Atoms, energy, compute and time are all bounded. So the useful question is not
what everyone can have, but what floor everyone is guaranteed.

## In his words

> No one can have anything. Everything is limited. Materials are limited. Atoms
> are limited. Access to energy is limited, and it will continue to be limited
> for many years. Compute is limited. Time is very limited.
> from: 2026-09-01-abundance-is-conditional

> The number of bottles of Cabernet Sauvignon of a special reserve of, I don't
> know, 15, 20 years ago, will be limited. You can't make more of those.
> from: 2026-09-01-abundance-is-conditional

## Stories and evidence

**The Cabernet** is the best image in the rant. Some scarcity is not a supply
problem to be solved but a fact about time. No amount of intelligence or energy
produces another bottle of a 20 year old reserve. Time is the one input that
does not yield to the technology.

**Even cures are rationed.** "Even if we start curing everything, the number of
cures available will be limited." Abundance in a category does not mean
unlimited within it.

## Caveats and edges

He immediately concedes the hard part: "defining the thresholds of what a good
lifestyle is for humanity as a whole, that's close to impossible." He poses the
problem without claiming to solve it.

## Gaps

  that conditions are national?
