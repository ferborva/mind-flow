# Storms can be national or shared across countries

## His words

> Each country could/would have their own "storm"/crisis  moments in isolation or there could be a broader one hitting globally or semi-globally at the same time.

Source: [his September 10 answer](../capture/2026-09-10-storms-as-social-contract-shifts.md#his-answer-verbatim).

**Fernando allows both isolated national events and shared events.** Country
breadth therefore matters alongside the Australian worked example at depth.

## What this does not settle

The time window for co-occurrence, how many countries make an event regional
or global, causal links between events, or a cross-country aggregation rule.
Treating shared candidates as co-occurrence of national candidates and never
summing their affected shares is Ren's commissioned operational reading.
