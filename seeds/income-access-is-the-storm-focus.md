# Access to means of generating income is the storm focus

## His words

> Those could be good overall as it's a larger monitoring surface area. However, I would simplify it by access to means to generate income as that is a fundamental key to access many others and be part of the social fabric

Source: [his September 10 clarification](../capture/2026-09-10-storms-as-social-contract-shifts.md#his-clarification-verbatim).

**Fernando narrows the monitoring focus to income-generating access.** He
relates this to security, food, work, education and health in the preceding
captured sentence; the simplification does not claim those needs are identical.

## What this does not settle

Which indicators represent disrupted access, how severe disruption must be,
or which condition category binds. Unemployment, underutilisation, wages,
poverty and other candidate series are Ren's operational proposals. Household
dependants, informal work, hours and job quality need their own evidence;
income-access language does not make those observable in every series.
