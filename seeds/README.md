# seeds

One idea per file. The unit of thought between a conversation and a piece.

A seed is a single claim, plus everything attached to it: the stories that
illustrate it, the caveats, the counter-examples, the direct quotes worth
keeping intact. It gathers material from across captures — a seed opened in
March can keep growing in November when the same idea comes up again.

A seed is not a draft. It has no shape, no opening, no argument. It is a pile
of evidence for one point, organised enough that a draft can be written from it.

**When a seed is ready to become a draft:** it has a claim that is actually
his, at least one concrete story or example, and enough substance that writing
it would not require inventing anything. A seed that keeps growing without
ripening is worth flagging in `meta/backlog.md` as a question to ask him.
