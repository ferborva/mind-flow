# Five percent is the proposed storm scale

## His words

> let's say a change that can affect 5% of society in a country or more.

Source: [his September 10 answer](../capture/2026-09-10-storms-as-social-contract-shifts.md#his-answer-verbatim).

**This is Fernando's proposed scale of effect.** It is not a measured affected
share, a probability or a calibrated warning threshold.

## What this does not settle

The population denominator, affected-person definition, assessment period or
persistence. His household-dependants follow-up remains unanswered. Converting
the scale into a change of five percentage points of population is a
commissioned operational reading, not a quotation or his settled measurement
rule. A relative five-percent change in a series is a different quantity.
