# Agency is the goal, conditions are the signals

Google's goal / signal / metric framework, pointed at abundance. **The goal is
human agency, and it is not measurable. The conditions are the signals, and they
are.** Not "how much agency does this person have", but what fraction of people
are above the price of a decent life, how many countries permit the thing, what
the coverage is. Actions sit below the signals, and every action is a hypothesis
that it moves one.

## In his words

> It very quickly brings me to the goal signal metric framework. The goal, in
> this case, is something that we could clearly understand as the abundant
> future, or the future of extreme agency for humanity. That is a goal. It's not
> something that we're gonna measure based on agency itself, it would be very
> hard to see on a progressive curve, especially since it's just very hard to
> measure in real time. However, we can indeed diagnose signals as we go, and
> those signals can be obtained from load bearing conditions, and those can
> evolve over time. So the signals can tell us how we're progressing towards
> that goal. And then below that, we're gonna be taking actions, defining
> actions and progressing towards them under the understanding that the actions
> we take will indeed move the needle on the signals that we are monitoring. At
> least that's what we're gonna build our hypotheses on top of.

## Stories and evidence

**This is the thing the observatory has been missing.** The Seldon Observatory
already tracks ten signals and has no stated theory of why those signals and not
others. GSM supplies it: agency is the goal, each panel is a load-bearing
condition, and a signal earns its place by being the measurable shadow of one.
See `dashboard/README.md` and `research/2026-09-07-red-team-the-observatory.md`.

**It also answers why the abundance conversation stays vibes.** The goal is
genuinely unmeasurable, so a conversation aimed straight at it has nothing to
check itself against. Conditions are countable and they move.

**And it inherits the recursive move.** The supplier's own if becomes a signal
you can track to know whether a free service is real or a phase. See
`run-the-frame-on-the-supplier`.

## Caveats and edges

**Signals get gamed and mistaken for the goal.** Cheap compute everywhere while
housing eats the gain shows green on the dashboard and changes nothing, which is
the Baumol problem arriving through the metrics layer.

**Choosing which conditions count as signals is a values judgement dressed as
measurement.** It has to be declared rather than hidden, and the current panel
selection is undeclared.

## Gaps

- Which conditions are actually measurable today? This is where the frame either
  becomes concrete or stays a schema, and he did not answer it.
- Confirm the framework's provenance and name before publishing it as Google's.
- Does the metrics layer belong in piece one at all, or is it observatory
  machinery? Current plan says the piece stays at the reframe.
