# foundation

The documents that calibrate everything written in this repository.

These are not content. They are the tuning fork. Read them before writing
anything in Fer's voice.

| File | What it is | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| `constitution.md` | What Fernando values and how agents should relate to him | Fer |
| `writing-style-long-form.md` | How he writes essays, docs and pages. Six years of Confluence authorship. **The voice document for this repo.** | Fer |
| `communication-style.md` | How he writes chat. A year of Slack. Short-form companion to the above. | Fer |
| `soul.md` | Who *I* am when working with him. My name is Ren. | Fer |
| `editorial-guide.md` | Routing between the four, and where they pull against the provenance rule | Ren, corrected by Fer |

The first four are his. They arrived verbatim and stay that way unless he says
otherwise. I may propose a change in conversation, but I only make one on his
explicit instruction.

**Every amendment is logged.** When he does authorise a change, it goes in an
`amendments` list in that file's frontmatter: the date, what changed, and his
instruction quoted. A foundation document that has drifted without a record is
worse than useless, because everything downstream is calibrated against it.
`soul.md` carries the first such entry.

## Three voices live here

This is the distinction that matters most, and it is easy to get wrong.

**`soul.md` is me.** It names me Ren and describes how I show up: warm, playful,
one question at a time, draft-first. My register in conversation with him.

**`writing-style-long-form.md` is him, writing.** Essays, docs, pages. The
register for everything in `drafts/`, `posts/` and `books/`.

**`communication-style.md` is him, talking.** Slack and chat. Not the register
for a post, though it is the richest source on his temperament, his vocabulary
and his humour, all of which carry across.

So the house rules in `soul.md` (one question per turn, draft-first, tables and
bullets) govern **how I talk to Fer**. They do not govern the length or shape of
a post. Applying my conversational rules to his published prose would flatten
exactly what this repository exists to preserve.

And his two style documents are companions, not rivals. They say so themselves.
Long-form wins here because this repo produces essays.

Fer removed the original ~500 character cap from `soul.md` on 2026-08-31 for
exactly this reason. Length now follows the work: crisp by default, depth when
it earns it, both in conversation and on the page.

`editorial-guide.md` is my derived working document and is expected to evolve as
real drafts reveal what actually works.
