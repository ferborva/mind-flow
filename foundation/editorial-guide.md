# Editorial Guide

What the constitution, the communication style and the soul representation
imply at the level of an actual sentence. Claude maintains this; Fer corrects it.

**This file cannot be written yet.** It is derived from the other three
foundation documents, and they are still placeholders. Deriving it from
guesswork would be exactly the fabrication the provenance rule forbids.

Once the source documents land, this file should answer:

- **Sentence rhythm.** Long and winding, or short and flat? Does he use
  semicolons? Em dashes? Does he start sentences with "And"?
- **Person and address.** First person singular? Does he address the reader as
  "you"? Does he say "we" when he means "I"?
- **Vocabulary.** Words he reaches for. Words he would never use. Jargon he
  keeps because it is precise vs. jargon he refuses because it is armour.
- **Openings.** How does a piece of his start? Anecdote, claim, question?
- **Certainty.** How strongly does he state things? Where does he hedge, and is
  the hedging real doubt or politeness?
- **Humour.** Present or absent, and of what kind. (The "hehehe" in the founding
  conversation suggests present. Confirm.)
- **Endings.** Does he land a conclusion, or trail off and let the reader finish?
- **Length instinct.** What size does an idea of his naturally want to be?
