# IMF WEO retained evidence

Source: International Monetary Fund, World Economic Outlook database, April
2026, https://data.imf.org/Datasets/WEO. © IMF. Original workbook, publisher
landing page and methodological appendix are retained unchanged with exact HTTP
response headers and SHA-256 hashes in `capture.json`.

These are publisher bytes, not Ren-authored prose. The workbook's country notes,
estimates, definitions, exceptional reporting periods and other qualifications
remain part of the evidence. `country-set.v1.json` is a filtered and sorted
derivative. No IMF endorsement is implied.

## Rights status and acquisition limits

The legacy terms URL returned an empty HTML body with a Refresh destination,
not substantive terms. Its bytes and headers are preserved as failed evidence.
Direct attempts at the destination returned HTTP 403; those headers are also
retained. Do not mistake HTTP 200 on the legacy redirect page for a licence.

On 10 September 2026, browsing verified the official
[Copyright and Usage](https://www.imf.org/en/about/copyright-and-terms) page,
effective 11 October 2024. Its special Data section explicitly includes WEO and
permits downloading, extraction, derivatives and distribution subject to accurate
IMF attribution, integrity and communication of terms. It separately requests
permission for potential commercial reuse and notes third-party exceptions.
General restrictions also remain. This is not a Creative Commons licence.

The substantive terms body was not acquired as exact local publisher bytes.
The capture therefore keeps specific permission unverified rather than claiming
a retained open licence. This one-off research proposal is not commercial reuse
clearance. No permission request was sent. Reusers must review the linked terms
and any third-party restrictions for their intended use.

Local request-start/end times were not instrumented. `retained_on` records the
local retention date and HTTP Date headers remain provider clocks, not proof of
authenticated acquisition or first publication.
