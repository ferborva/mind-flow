# Internet-use candidate source evidence

Source: International Telecommunication Union (ITU), World Telecommunication/ICT
Indicators Database, via World Bank World Development Indicators,
https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS.

`metadata.body` explicitly identifies `IT.NET.USER.ZS`, annual frequency,
percentage-of-population units, CC BY 4.0 and the requested attribution to ITU.
See https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/int/public-licenses#cc-by.
The original complete metadata retains the definition, methodology, exceptions
and country-comparability limits. Receipts hash both body and serialised headers.

Body bytes are exact after HTTP content decoding. Header serialization is
explicitly described in each receipt and is not wire-format evidence.
Publisher identity and global first publication are not independently verified.
`capability-candidate.v1.json` is a filtered and sorted derivative, not a publisher
product. It preserves source selectors and does not introduce imputed values.
