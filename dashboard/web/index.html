03 · Conditional paths

Possible futures, not a single destination.

These horizons are unscored hypotheses. They make the intended promise discussable while keeping competing explanations, adverse paths and democratic choices open.

HORIZON A · UNSCORED

Time returns

If productivity gains reach workers through shorter hours, stronger income or public provision, people gain time without losing security.

HORIZON B · UNSCORED

Capability reaches everyone

If price, eligibility, quality and delivery barriers fall together, useful services become genuinely accessible.

HORIZON C · UNSCORED

Choices multiply

If access is durable and power remains contestable, people can choose more of how they live, learn, care and contribute.

Possible failure modes

These are hypotheses to investigate, not diagnoses, probabilities or predictions of public behaviour.

Methods

What could change this reading?

A useful instrument names evidence that could narrow, reverse or redistribute its interpretation.

Bounded falsifiers

  • Transmission: matched cohort evidence showing earnings, security and hours improving with output would weaken the concern for that cohort and period.
  • Access: service-level evidence showing lower total cost, sustained quality and broader uptake would support a path from capability to lived access.
  • Agency: repeated affected-person evidence showing expanded voluntary choices without material dependency would support the agency condition.
  • Attribution: credible natural experiments or other causal designs could distinguish technology effects from demand, policy, composition and measurement effects.
