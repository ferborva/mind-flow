# books

Where the collection turns into something longer.

A book starts as a folder the moment five or so posts cluster around one theme:

```
books/<book-slug>/
  outline.md      # the arc: what it argues, in what order, what is missing
  chapters/       # chapters, drafted from posts and from new material
```

`outline.md` comes first and matters most. It is the honest state of the book:
which chapters exist, which are stubs, and — most usefully — which gaps in the
argument still need a conversation with Fer to fill. A book outline is the
strongest generator of good backlog questions in this repo.

Chapters are not posts pasted together. A post stands alone; a chapter assumes
the ones before it. Expect real rewriting when a post becomes a chapter, and
keep the post intact where it is.

No rush here. A book that emerges from the material is worth more than one
declared up front and then filled in.
