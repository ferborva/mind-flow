# Round 4 claim and evidence audit

> **Agent audit. Not a finding, forecast or public-release approval.** This
> review tests whether the programme's most important claims fit the evidence
> cited for them. Sources and wording require human and domain review before
> public use.

## Decision

The strongest defensible thesis is methodological:

> **Technical capability does not become human agency automatically. For a
> defined population, outcome, standard, place and period, a candidate route
> depends on observable conditions. Those conditions can be named, challenged,
> monitored and revised before consequential action.**

The current evidence does not establish that:

- an abundance transition is imminent, inevitable or desirable for everyone;
- the complete condition set is already known;
- one binding IF usually exists;
- money and permission are universally the dominant conditions;
- the candidate crisis-point set is exhaustive;
- an Observatory view diagnoses causes; or
- any current synthetic threshold justifies public intervention.

The programme should agree that abundance is conditional and reject the claim
that its conditions are already known, singular or sufficient. It becomes
credible when it can discover that its own map is wrong.

## Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Primary evidence directly supports the bounded statement. |
| **B** | Strong evidence with a material model, observational or scope limit. |
| **C** | A useful hypothesis, analogy or proposed transfer. |
| **D** | Unsupported, misclassified or contradicted in its current form. |
| **U** | Untested by the programme. |

These grades attach to the exact claim, not to a source's general quality.

## P0 claim dispositions

| Claim | Grade | Disposition | Defensible replacement |
|---|---:|---|---|
| The grammar discovers the binding IF. | U | Narrow and test. | It elicits a **candidate condition set for a registered route**. It becomes diagnostic only after counterfactual or intervention evidence. |
| `verb + blank + IF` is sufficient for public reasoning. | U | Expand. | Use **[WHO] can [VERB] [OUTCOME] to [STANDARD], in [PLACE], during [PERIOD], IF [CONDITION SET].** |
| Money and permission are the two largest IFs. | D as a general claim | Withdraw. | Treat affordability, eligibility, time, geography, capacity, quality and completed access as service-specific candidates. |
| About 80% below $30/day identifies who abundance rhetoric excludes. | D for that inference | Separate statistic and interpretation. | The user-selected threshold estimates a distribution under one World Bank-derived series and vintage. It does not identify the audience for abundance claims or the operative condition for a service. |
| The 2026 $8.30/day value is observed. | B | Relabel as a nowcast. | Post-2024 values in the cited March 2026 vintage are modelled nowcasts, not direct observations. |
| Corporate profit can fund a bridge without public machinery. | D for the mechanism | Withdraw. | Profit figures illustrate scale only. Taxable capacity, incidence, jurisdiction, behavioural response, delivery and authority require separate evidence. |
| Goldman Sachs reports observed displacement of 6% to 7%. | D | Correct category. | It is a research projection of possible transitional displacement, not an observed outcome. |
| IMF exposure means workers will lose jobs. | D for that inference | Correct category. | Occupational exposure is neither adoption, automation nor job loss. |
| National healthcare coverage means zero price and completed access. | D | Replace. | Name the programme, service, patient payment, eligibility, wait, geography, quality and completed outcome. |
| Five dated phases forecast the transition. | D | Archive as a scenario. | Use a state graph. Routes may overlap, reverse, split or never occur. |
| Price conditions will migrate to permission. | C | Register as a forecast candidate. | The condition may migrate, split, disappear or expose an omitted condition for a named service and cohort. |

## Primary-source ceilings

### Poverty and resources

The [World Bank Poverty and Inequality Platform](https://pip.worldbank.org/about)
and [Our World in Data $30 series](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/poverty-share-on-less-than-30-per-day)
can support a threshold-specific distribution estimate with its exact vintage,
international-dollar basis, survey mixture and nowcast status. The
[March 2026 World Bank update](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/march-2026-global-poverty-update-from-the-world-bank--new-data-a)
and [technical note](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099434403202630952/pdf/IDU-6226a428-a460-457e-ad48-9625630a029f.pdf)
explain that recent periods are modelled.

They cannot identify the audience for future rhetoric, personal wealth,
service eligibility, public provision, future prices or an individual's
effective access. Keep each inference smaller than the source.

### Work transition

The [IMF exposure analysis](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/006/2024/001/article-A001-en.xml)
supports a modelled occupational-exposure statement. The
[ILO 2025 update](https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-2025-update)
also models potential exposure and states that transformation is more likely
than replacement. Neither measures realised adoption, displacement, earnings
loss or Australian local effects.

The [Goldman Sachs discussion](https://www.goldmansachs.com/pdfs/insights/goldman-sachs-exchanges/ai-exchanges-ais-impact-on-employment/transcript.pdf)
provides a projection, not observed displacement. The public interface must
label projection, estimate and observation differently.

The [ABS Characteristics of Australian Business 2024-25](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/technology-and-innovation/characteristics-australian-business/2024-25)
supports the share of in-scope businesses reporting any AI use. It does not
supply use intensity, task exposure, worker linkage or causal outcome.

### Access and public provision

The [OECD Health at a Glance 2025](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/11/health-at-a-glance-2025_a894f72e/8f9e3f98-en.pdf)
and [2025 universal-health-coverage monitoring report](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/universalhealthcoverage/publication/2025-global-monitoring-report-gmr)
treat access as multidimensional. A coverage or reimbursement statistic cannot
stand in for zero out-of-pocket cost, acceptable waiting time, suitable quality
or a completed outcome.

The programme should measure the whole route: eligibility, money cost, time
cost, distance, capacity, quality, continuity, comprehension, refusal and
appeal.

### Distribution and fiscal routes

Company accounts such as the
[Alphabet 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000018/goog-20251231.htm)
and [Nvidia 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/ixviewer/ix.html?doc=%2FArchives%2Fedgar%2Fdata%2F1045810%2F000104581026000021%2Fnvda-20260125.htm)
can establish reported company income. They do not establish a lawful,
efficient or equitable distribution mechanism.

The [IMF fiscal-policy note](https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/Staff-Discussion-Notes/Issues/2024/06/11/Broadening-the-Gains-from-Generative-AI-The-Role-of-Fiscal-Policies-549639)
supports treating transfers, public services and tax reform as policy options.
The programme should also retain wage insurance, bargaining, ownership,
competition and reduced-work routes. No one route receives default status.

### Measurement beyond market prices

The [OECD review of GDP in a digitalised economy](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/measuring-gdp-in-a-digitalised-economy_5jlwqd81d09r-en.html)
finds the framework broadly capable while acknowledging practical issues. The
[US BEA FAQ](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/1250) explains the treatment of free,
advertising-supported services. A
[BLS measurement paper](https://www.bls.gov/osmr/research-papers/2025/pdf/ec250020.pdf)
discusses free goods outside conventional CPI scope.

The defensible statement is that conventional accounts capture much digital
production, while free services, consumer surplus, quality change and
distribution remain difficult to interpret. Claims that GDP and CPI become
blind, or that one device contains a precise replacement value, are too strong.

### Early-warning transfer

The [UNDRR global multi-hazard review](https://www.undrr.org/reports/global-status-MHEWS-2024)
supports associations between warning systems and better disaster outcomes. It
does not establish causal portability to technological transition. The
[IFRC anticipatory-action pillar](https://www.ifrc.org/happening-now/emergencies/anticipatory-pillar-dref)
supports a useful design pattern: pre-agreed triggers, actions, finance and
stock.

Use that as an analogy to test, not borrowed validation. An Observatory warning
still needs demonstrated lead time, discrimination, prepared capacity, a
legitimate owner and a safe last-mile route.

### Australian local rehearsal

[Jobs and Skills Australia's NERO](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/nero)
is a modelled, smoothed and revision-prone occupation-by-region series. Its
[methodology](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/nero/nero-methodology) and
[version 3 technical paper](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-06/nowcast_of_employment_by_region_and_occupation_3.0_.pdf)
support point-estimate evaluation, not validated warning precision,
sensitivity, causal attribution or intervention value.

The frozen archive is a retrospective **signal replay**, not a historical
warning backtest. A warning backtest requires the information and revision
vintage available at each decision date, rolling-origin evaluation and an
outcome-linked score. The October 2027 Census benchmark may test point or rank
accuracy. It cannot alone validate AI attribution, household harm, warning lead
time or intervention utility.

### Ecological and national context

The [IEA Energy and AI report](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/executive-summary)
supports global sector projections and bottleneck questions. Local grid, water,
price and community effects need project-level evidence.

The [World Bank Digital Progress and Trends 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/dptr2025-ai-foundations/report)
offers connectivity, compute, context and competency as useful dimensions. Do
not compress them into an unauditable country verdict or treat them as a
complete causal model.

## Red-team the red team

The existing red teams correctly challenge inevitability, distributional
blindness and metric failure. They weaken themselves when they use promotional
anecdotes as counterevidence, treat institutional metaphors as empirical
mechanisms, or offer Universal Basic AI and incumbent destruction as settled
remedies.

For every headline claim, the stronger red-team record contains:

1. the strongest rival explanation;
2. the observation that would distinguish them;
3. the decision affected;
4. the people and losses omitted by both accounts; and
5. what remains unknown after the test.

The goal is not a sharper opposing story. It is a discriminating test.

## Forecast validation rules

1. Freeze event wording, target metric, scope, PERIOD, data vintage, resolution
   bytes and resolver before issue.
2. Keep current IF state, future probability and action authority on separate
   axes.
3. Require naive and reference-class baselines.
4. Use rolling-origin evaluation, following
   [Forecasting: Principles and Practice](https://otexts.com/fpp3/tscv.html),
   before claiming historical performance.
5. Use proper scores that reward honest probabilities, as reviewed by
   [Gneiting and Raftery](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf).
6. Aggregate duplicates by underlying event or dependency cluster.
7. Publish sample counts and uncertainty. Do not call a sparse reliability
   curve calibrated. See the
   [ECMWF reliability guidance](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=311146420).
8. Describe any minimum count or score threshold as a programme rule unless it
   has independent empirical justification.

## Public falsification agenda

The programme should publish failure conditions before promoting the method:

1. Independent encoders cannot reproduce condition sets reliably.
2. Important omitted conditions repeatedly appear after decisions.
3. IF cards do not improve comprehension or appropriate action over a simpler
   release.
4. Warning rules fail to beat naive baselines after revision and dependency
   correction.
5. Warning lead time is shorter than preparation lead time.
6. A proposed condition changes without the predicted outcome changing.
7. Removing a supposed constraint does not improve the registered outcome.
8. Condition relief shifts serious harm to another cohort or place.
9. Affected people reject the chosen outcome, standard or loss rule.
10. No authorised and funded actor can use the signal.
11. A simpler communication performs as well or better.
12. The programme selectively publishes favourable forecasts or silently
    changes definitions.

Null, adverse and contradictory results are programme outputs. They are not
failed marketing.

## Immediate evidence work

1. Create an atomic claim record for every P0 row above.
2. Replace fixed transition dates with registered, resolvable event forecasts.
3. Keep NERO in no-consequence prospective replay until warning performance is
   demonstrated.
4. Pre-register the independent-encoder and 2 by 2 comprehension studies.
5. Turn every candidate crisis crossing into an arrow-level forecast with a
   rival explanation and safe preparation lead time.
6. Publish source, scope, uncertainty, falsifier and authority before visual
   drama.
7. Publish every correction, void, expiry and retired hypothesis.
