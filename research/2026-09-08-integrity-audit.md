# Phase 0 evidence integrity audit for the abundance transition

> **Provenance warning:** This is Ren's evidence audit. It is not Fernando's
> opinion and does not imply his endorsement. It evaluates claims already in the
> repository and proposes tests. Any change in Fernando's position must be
> captured in a later conversation before it becomes his substance.

## 🦅 TL;DR

- **The project should test how capability becomes real human agency, not set
  out to prove that abundance is inevitable.** The Observatory is strongest as
  a decision system that can invalidate its own thesis.
- One published calculation must stop at the line now. The estimate that
  lifting everyone below $30 a day costs 62% of global GDP treats every person
  below the line as if they had zero resources. The required calculation is the
  aggregate shortfall, not the full threshold multiplied by the headcount.
- Two headline equations are useful prose but not valid measures as written.
  Money does not reach households only through wages and prices, and five
  unlike access dimensions cannot be multiplied without a shared probabilistic
  denominator.
- The next proof should be narrow: **Australia, clerical workers, a continuity
  floor of essential services, and a trace from AI exposure through adoption,
  worker outcomes and household access.**
- Every path now needs an IF register: scope, condition type, state, evidence,
  counter-hypothesis, falsifier, threshold, owner, response and review date.

`evidence/claim-ledger-2026-09-08.json`.

## 🎯 The proposition under test

The current material contains two different projects that should not be allowed
to blur together.

1. **A public argument:** technological capability is not the same thing as
   access. Every promise of agency carries conditions.
2. **A transition instrument:** observe those conditions, forecast breaches and
   attach preparation and protection before harm compounds.

The public argument is strong enough to motivate the instrument. It is not
strong enough to predetermine its answer. A defensible research objective is:

> **Test whether valuable capabilities are becoming reliably accessible to more
> people, with greater choice, while distributional, institutional, operational
> and ecological failures are detected early enough to act.**

This formulation allows abundance to advance, stall, reverse or arrive unevenly.
It makes agency an outcome rather than a synonym for technical capability.

## 🛑 Claims that stop at the line

### 1. The global uplift arithmetic

The repository currently estimates the annual cost of lifting 6.64 billion
people to $30 per day as:

> 6.64bn people × $30 × 365 = $72.7tn

That is the cost only if all 6.64 billion people start at zero. People are
classified below a line because their welfare measure falls somewhere below it.
The relevant statistic is the sum of each person's shortfall. The World Bank
calls the corresponding population statistic the poverty gap and defines it as
the mean shortfall from the line, counting the non-poor as having no shortfall.

**Disposition: withdraw the 62% claim until it is recomputed from the welfare
distribution.** The corrected estimate must state whether each country's welfare
aggregate is income or consumption, the PPP vintage, interpolation or nowcast
method, survey coverage and uncertainty.

This correction does not show that redistribution is sufficient. It shows that
the current number cannot establish that it is insufficient by a factor of
twenty.

### 2. Cost collapse as the only mechanism

Even with corrected arithmetic, the conclusion that only falling cost can close
the gap does not follow. Access can improve through a portfolio of:

- productivity and earnings growth
- transfers and taxation
- public or common provision
- broader capital ownership
- reduced required working time
- lower consumer cost
- changes in eligibility, capacity, quality and delivery

**Disposition: replace exclusivity with a comparative hypothesis.** The testable
question is which combination increases effective access fastest, for whom, at
what fiscal and social cost, and with what resilience.

### 3. Negative CPI as a requirement

A target service can become cheaper in quality-adjusted terms while aggregate
consumer prices still rise. Headline CPI combines many categories, changes
weights over time and is designed to measure price change in household
expenditure, not the number of capabilities made possible by technology.

**Disposition: do not treat positive aggregate inflation as failure.** Measure
the full cost of reaching a defined outcome, including money, time and required
complements, service by service.

### 4. Labour participation as choice

Participation changes with ageing, education, disability, discouraged work,
care obligations, policy and migration. It cannot answer whether people are
choosing not to work because their needs are securely met.

**Disposition: remove the interpretation, retain the series only as context.**
The choice hypothesis needs desired and actual hours, reasons for
non-participation, income security and cohort transitions.

## 🧮 Measures that need reconstruction

### The transmission chain

The sentence "money reaches people through wages or prices" is memorable but
incomplete. The later draft already names some omitted channels. The measurable
chain should be:

> capability → economic feasibility → adoption → task or service redesign →
> labour and price effects → household resources and service provision →
> effective access → outcomes → agency and consent

Transfers, self-employment, capital income, taxes, debt, wealth, public and
household production, unpaid care, time cost and environmental cost can all
alter that chain. Every arrow contains IF conditions. Exposure is not adoption;
adoption is not automation; automation is not displacement; nominal access is
not effective access.

### The access equation

Availability, affordability, eligibility, quality and delivery are defensible
dimensions. Multiplication is not defensible until they are measured as
conditional probabilities for the same person, service, period and need.
Independent percentages from different datasets cannot be multiplied into an
access probability.

Use a service-level funnel instead:

1. people who need or value the outcome
2. people for whom sufficient capacity is available
3. people eligible under the actual rules
4. people able to bear the money and time cost
5. people reached through a usable delivery route
6. people receiving safe, effective and acceptable quality
7. people achieving the intended outcome

Report the count and rate lost at every transition. The narrowest stage is the
observed binding IF. This pattern resembles WHO's mature distinction between
service coverage, effective quality and financial protection, without assuming
that a health index transfers unchanged to every service.

### The zero-cost count

A count rewards trivial additions and ignores scale, outcome, quality and costs
shifted to advertisers, public budgets, personal data, attention, unpaid labour
or the environment. It can rise while meaningful access falls.

Replace it provisionally with **verified effective-access coverage** for a named
service and cohort. Publish consumer money cost, time cost, total provision
cost, funding route, quality, reach and outcome separately. Whether something is
called "free" then becomes an attribute, not the target.

### The current Engels comparison

The Observatory's derived series is useful as an aggregate distributional
baseline. It is not a like-for-like replay of Allen's historical comparison:

- GDP per capita is not output per worker.
- labour share times real GDP per capita is aggregate real labour income per
  person, not a cohort wage series.
- the GDP deflator measures production prices, not household purchasing power.
- global aggregation can change through country composition.
- modelled labour-share estimates and recent projections carry uncertainty and
  revisions.

**Disposition: retain with a neutral name and a decomposition.** Do not call a
3.5-point global difference one tenth of an Engels' Pause until both periods
have been reconstructed on comparable definitions with intervals.

## 🧩 Turn every IF into a testable object

The conditions are the most original part of the thesis. A flat list is not yet
a decision system. Conditions can be necessary, sufficient, enabling,
inhibiting or merely correlated. They can combine through AND, OR and dependency
relationships, and they can feed back into each other.

Every IF record should contain:

| Field | Question it forces |
|---|---|
| Outcome | What verb and result are being promised? |
| Scope | For which people, service, place and period? |
| Condition class | Technical, physical, economic, distributional, permission, delivery, human, ecological or geopolitical? |
| Logical role | Necessary, sufficient, enabling, inhibiting or correlated? |
| State | Unknown, blocked, fragile, satisfied or regressing? |
| Evidence | Which leading, confirming and counter-signals bear on it? |
| Uncertainty | Sampling, model, revision, definition, causality and data age? |
| Decision rule | What threshold matters, and what are the costs of a miss or false alarm? |
| Action contract | Who prepares, protects and recovers, with which authority and budget? |
| Falsifier | What observed result would change the model? |
| Review | When does the claim expire or get reconsidered? |

This is a conditional graph, not a maturity score. The Observatory should be
able to show which IF changed state, what evidence moved it, which paths it
opens or closes and which action that change authorises.

## 🧪 First validation programme

### Pilot boundary

- **Country:** Australia
- **Cohort:** general clerks, accounting clerks and contact-centre workers
- **Outcome floor:** continuity of housing, food, energy, transport, primary
  healthcare and connectivity
- **Geography:** state or SA4 where data permits
- **Comparison:** matched workers in occupations with similar pre-period trends
  and lower realised AI adoption

Jobs and Skills Australia supplies a 2025 task-level map of augmentation and
automation exposure and identifies clerical occupations among the most exposed.
The Australian Bureau of Statistics reports actual business AI use for 2024-25,
including industry and business-size cuts. That permits the first essential
separation: potential exposure versus observed adoption.

The pilot must say where the data fails. Selected Living Cost Indexes distinguish
household types but are national. Detailed Household Expenditure Survey weights
remain partly anchored to 2015-16. High-frequency linked worker and household
outcomes may require restricted microdata or a research partnership.

### Validation sequence

1. **Reproduce and correct.** Recompute every load-bearing number from its
   primary source and preserve the exact vintage.
2. **Define before scoring.** Agree the outcome floor and IF records with the
   affected cohort, statisticians and service operators.
3. **Establish the baseline.** Join exposure, adoption, worker flows, household
   resources, service use and outcomes without causal claims.
4. **Test construct validity.** Ask whether the measures identify households
   that people and frontline services recognise as losing access.
5. **Backtest warnings.** Use earlier automation, industry closure, pandemic
   and cost-of-living episodes. Measure lead time, misses and false alarms.
6. **Test attribution.** Use matched comparisons, event studies or stronger
   designs where adoption timing permits. Publish alternative explanations.
7. **Issue forecasts.** Start with adoption, worker-flow and access breaches,
   not a probability of social collapse. Store forecasts at issuance.
8. **Score forecasts.** Compare with naive baselines; use error scores for
   quantities and Brier scores plus reliability diagrams for threshold events.
9. **Rehearse the response.** Time registry reach, payment, service capacity and
   appeal resolution in a simulated concentrated displacement shock.
10. **Scale only after failure analysis.** Add countries and services when the
    pilot's definitions, data and interventions survive external review.

## 📊 Uncertainty contract

Every signal should disclose separately:

- sampling error and population coverage
- modelling, imputation and nowcast error
- revision history and source vintage
- definitional and cross-country comparability
- structural and causal uncertainty
- timeliness, missingness and known directional bias

Composite scores require sensitivity analysis across inclusion, normalisation,
weighting, missing-data and aggregation choices. A risk colour must never be a
restyled data-coverage percentage. A scenario must never be called a forecast.
A probability must not be called calibrated until enough forecasts have been
resolved to test calibration.

## 🗣 Public narrative contract

Every update should answer, in order:

1. What did we observe?
2. Who appears affected, and how uncertain is that?
3. What remains an inference or scenario?
4. Which IF changed state?
5. What action does that condition authorise now?
6. What evidence would prove this reading wrong?
7. When will it be checked again?

The public page needs three layers: a plain-language result, an evidence card
and full methods. It should never call technical capability an outcome,
exposure a job loss, a national average a cohort result, instrumentation
coverage a risk score, or a scenario a forecast.

## ⚠️ What remains genuinely unknown

- Whether AI adoption causes net harm or net gain for the selected cohort.
- Whether price, income, provision, ownership or permission becomes the binding
  IF for each essential outcome.
- Whether any of the five proposed crises is forecastable with useful lead time.
- Whether pre-committed responses developed for other shocks transfer to an AI
  labour transition without capture or harmful false alarms.
- Whether a single public observatory improves understanding and agency rather
  than increasing anxiety or manufacturing legitimacy for disputed choices.

Those are not defects to hide. **They are the programme.**
