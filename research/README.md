# research

Externally sourced facts. **Not his words, and not my invention.**

This directory exists because the provenance rule needed a third category. The
repo already distinguished two:

1. **His substance**, which lives in `capture/` and must never be fabricated.
2. **My scaffolding**, the transitions and structure that carry no claim of his.

Research is a third thing: **verifiable third-party facts, attributed to their
source.** They are not his opinions, so quoting them does not breach the
provenance rule. But they are also not free-floating: every figure here carries
a source link and a retrieval date, and any figure that reaches a draft carries
its attribution with it.

## The rules

- **Every number has a source URL and a date.** No exceptions. An unsourced
  figure in this directory is a bug of exactly the same severity as an invented
  opinion in a post.
- **Say when a number is derived.** Arithmetic I did on top of sourced inputs is
  marked as such, with the inputs and the working shown, so it can be checked.
- **Say when a number is a range.** False precision is worse than an honest
  interval.
- **Research never becomes his opinion.** A fact can support, sharpen, or
  demolish one of his claims. It cannot become one. If the research changes what
  he thinks, that is a conversation, and the outcome lands in a new capture.
- **Record what contradicts him.** The point of doing this is to find out where
  he is wrong before a reader does. Findings that undercut his argument go in
  first, not last.

## What is in here

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `2026-09-07-abundance-figures.md` | Verifies or corrects the quantitative claims in the abundance rant |
| `2026-09-07-decent-life-thresholds.md` | What a decent life costs, and what capital funds it for a life and the next generation |
| `2026-09-07-red-team-moonshot.md` | The strongest counter-arguments to his thesis, argued from the actual published positions of the people he wants to persuade |

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`, dated by when the research was done, because facts go
stale and the date is how you know to re-check.
