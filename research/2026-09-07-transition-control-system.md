# Red team of the transition plan, and a control system for acting early

This is Ren's analysis, commissioned to strengthen `from-if-to-when`. It is not
Fer substance and should not be promoted into a post without his review.

## 🦅 TL;DR

- **The thesis survives:** abundance is not a capability forecast. It is a
  transmission problem, and access is the result to measure.
- **The transmission ratio does not yet survive:** a national labour-income
  share and a price index have different units, populations and time behaviour.
  Dividing one rate by the other produces a memorable number, not yet a reliable
  trigger.
- **The calendar should become a set of gates:** nobody knows whether a phase
  begins in 2028 or 2038. We can know whether displacement is accelerating,
  household access is falling, delivery systems are ready, and a promised floor
  is actually reachable.
- **ADKAR is useful for communication, not sufficient for political economy.**
  It tells us why different cohorts hear the same message differently. It does
  not tell us who pays, who has power, or what prevents capture.
- **Every warning needs a pre-committed response.** A dashboard without an owner,
  threshold and action is a weather report. The stronger proposal is a
  transition control system.

## ⚖️ What I would defend, concede and change

| Position | My take | Stronger formulation |
|---|---|---|
| Every abundance promise hides conditions | **Defend.** This is the original and durable insight. | Capability is not access. Name the conditions between them. |
| Money persists | **Narrow.** Cash may weaken while allocation, eligibility and scarcity remain. | The token may change. The allocation problem does not disappear. |
| Companies must choose | **Concede.** Voluntary virtue loses to incentives and competition. | Design a bargain that works when every actor is self-interested. |
| Reinvestment is enough | **Test.** It is enough only when gains reach exposed households through income, lower essential costs, transfers or public provision. | Measure the household access margin, not investment or productivity alone. |
| The binding if migrates to permission | **Keep as a hypothesis.** Permission may dominate digital services, while housing, energy, land and care remain material constraints. | Money may stop binding first in some baskets. Track which condition binds, by place and cohort. |
| Human-required work becomes impossible to staff | **Soften.** People also work for status, meaning and access to scarce wants. The reservation wage rises, it does not necessarily become infinite. | Expect essential human work to demand more pay, status, autonomy, rotation or civic obligation. |
| Engels' Pause forecasts the transition | **Reject as forecast, keep as warning.** AI may substitute for labour more broadly, while modern welfare states and democratic politics may compress the pain. | History proves a long transmission lag is possible. It does not date the next one. |

The bigger correction is to widen the channel model. **Market gains commonly
reach households through wages and prices, but societies also transmit them
through capital income, transfers and public provision.** The original two-way
test is still useful if it is explicitly a test of market transmission, not a
complete account of household welfare.

## 📏 Replace the one ratio with an access stack

The unit of success should be a person who can obtain a decent-living basket,
not a product whose marginal cost happens to approach zero.

> **Access = availability × affordability × eligibility × quality ×
> delivery. If any factor is zero, access is zero.**

That multiplication matters. A free diagnosis that is illegal locally, too
low-quality to trust, or unreachable without broadband does not count.

### The core measure: household access margin

For each representative household and exposed cohort:

> **Access margin = disposable resources + in-kind public provision - local
> cost of the decent-living basket.**

Disposable resources include wages, self-employment income, capital income and
transfers after tax. In-kind provision includes services the household can
actually use, valued consistently. Report the share of people with a margin at
or above zero, plus the size of the shortfall below zero.

This fixes four problems in the original ratio:

1. It compares money with money.
2. It includes transfers and public services without pretending they are wages.
3. It can be cut by cohort, region and household type.
4. It measures access even when a service is free to the consumer but costly to
   provide.

European reference budgets already define baskets of goods and services needed
for an acceptable standard of living by household and place. ILOSTAT already
publishes labour-income-share and distribution measures for 189 countries.
This is therefore an integration problem before it is a data-invention problem.

### The supporting dashboard

| Signal | Definition | Early-warning pattern | Pre-committed response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Access coverage** | Share of each cohort with access margin ≥ 0 | Falls for two releases while national productivity rises | Automatic bridge income or in-kind access for the affected cohort |
| **Transition speed** | Gross displacement, median time to re-employment, replacement earnings | Displacement accelerates and re-employment slows | Wage insurance, portable benefits, hiring-linked training |
| **Essential basket pressure** | Local reference-budget cost, split into automatable and human-required items | Human-required basket outruns disposable resources | Capacity plan, pay review, migration or credential reform |
| **Zero-cost access count** | Basket items meeting availability, affordability, eligibility, quality and delivery tests | Capability rises but count stays flat | Outcome-based procurement and access obligations |
| **Concentration** | Compute, energy and service capacity controlled by the largest providers | Rising concentration plus falling access coverage | Interoperability, procurement diversification, competition review |
| **Preparedness** | Registry coverage, payment lead time, appeal capacity, pre-positioned finance | Support cannot reach an exposed cohort within the policy target | Fund and rehearse delivery before expanding automation |
| **Trust and consent** | Repeated survey of fairness, confidence and willingness to support the transition | Trust falls before economic triggers breach | Publish uncertainty, open two-way forums, use trusted local messengers |
| **Cross-border access gap** | Variance in access coverage and eligibility across countries | Gap widens for an essential service | Mutual recognition, pooled procurement, portable eligibility |

The exact trigger values should be set publicly, locally and **before** they are
breached. Two consecutive releases is a useful anti-noise default, not a
universal law. Every measure also needs a distributional cut. A national average
can be green while a town, occupation or age group is already in crisis.

## 📣 Communication is part of the intervention

The draft diagnoses cohort desynchronisation but does not yet operationalise it.
The communication contract should be consistent even when the message changes:

1. **What changed?** Use observed facts, not capability theatre.
2. **Who is affected now?** Name occupations, places and uncertainty ranges.
3. **What has already triggered?** Announce the protection with the warning.
4. **What can I do this week?** Give one reachable action and one place for help.
5. **When is the next update?** A fixed cadence prevents an information vacuum.

| Cohort | What they need to hear | Proof that makes it credible | Useful action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Not yet exposed** | What is being measured and what would trigger action | Public baseline and rules that bind government and business | Check personal exposure and benefit portability |
| **Exposed, still employed** | What may change, what is unknown, and what protection exists | Employer transition plan and independent sector data | Build options before displacement, without panic |
| **Displaced** | You are not being asked to celebrate the technology that harmed you | Benefit activation, case owner and appeal route | Stabilise income and housing first, then choose a pathway |
| **Essential workers** | Your bargaining power and social value are recognised | Pay, staffing ratios, autonomy and credential changes | Co-design the new employment bargain |
| **Wider public** | What society is gaining, losing and learning | Same dashboard, including red indicators and failed pilots | Participate in local priority-setting |

Three rules follow. **Never announce abundance beside an unprotected loss. Never
hide uncertainty. Never publish a warning without the action it activates.**
WHO emergency guidance supports the underlying pattern: information should be
early, repeated, trusted, accessible and actionable, with affected communities
as partners rather than audiences.

## ⚡ Five crisis points, forecast as conditions

### 1. The credibility break

**Condition:** capability headlines and productivity rise while the household
access margin is flat or falling.

**What forms:** rejection of abundance language, distrust of official measures,
and fertile ground for anyone who can name a villain.

**Act before it:** publish the baseline, uncertainties and activation rules.
Replace destination rhetoric with household evidence.

### 2. The local displacement cascade

**Condition:** losses cluster in an occupation or town, re-employment time rises,
and replacement earnings fall even while national employment looks healthy.

**What forms:** sectoral mobilisation, anti-automation politics and geographic
decline hidden by national averages.

**Act before it:** trigger portable benefits and wage insurance automatically.
Fund training only where employers commit to interviews or jobs. Protect housing
and healthcare continuity before asking people to retrain.

### 3. The legitimacy break

**Condition:** access coverage falls while profits, mark-ups or infrastructure
concentration rise.

**What forms:** windfall-tax campaigns, break-up proposals, nationalisation and
direct action against infrastructure.

**Act before it:** establish access obligations, transparent cost curves,
independent audit and a public return for public subsidy. The bargain must be
visible while conditions are good.

### 4. The essential-work squeeze

**Condition:** vacancy duration, exit rates and service queues rise together in
care, trades, education or other human-required work.

**What forms:** strikes, emergency migration, informal rationing and conflict
over who owes society necessary labour.

**Act before it:** improve pay, status, autonomy and staffing before coercive
options enter the debate. Build credential portability and train reserve capacity.

### 5. The permission and border split

**Condition:** an essential service becomes technically cheap but access varies
sharply by identity, jurisdiction, language or certification.

**What forms:** migration pressure, digital sovereignty blocs, black markets and
politics around deserving access.

**Act before it:** negotiate mutual recognition, portable eligibility and a
minimum access compact. Keep local control above the floor. Make the floor
interoperable across borders.

## 🧭 Preparation by actor

### Individuals and households

- Map income concentration risk: employer, occupation, geography and benefits.
- Make qualifications and work evidence portable before disruption.
- Know the local support, appeal and retraining routes before they are needed.
- Build relationships and mutual-aid capacity. A household plan without a
  community layer is fragile.

This is resilience, not an instruction to predict a career twenty years out.
Individuals cannot diversify away a system-wide shock, and the burden must not
be quietly transferred to them.

### Communities, unions and civil society

- Maintain a local transition map: exposed employers, essential services,
  trusted messengers and delivery gaps.
- Negotiate data access and transition terms before redundancies.
- Pre-agree mutual aid, legal support and rapid feedback channels.
- Test national dashboard claims against lived access and publish the gap.

### Businesses

- Publish service cost and access curves, including quality and distribution.
- File a worker-transition plan before a material automation deployment.
- Pre-fund portable benefits or wage insurance where displacement is foreseeable.
- Accept independent audit in exchange for regulatory certainty and procurement.

### Countries

- Build the delivery rail first: registry, payments, casework, appeals, finance
  and privacy protection. World Bank adaptive social protection treats these as
  infrastructure that must exist before a shock.
- Legislate triggers and owners for each response. Run simulations annually.
- Publish cohort and regional cuts, not only national averages.
- Use outcome-based procurement to buy verified access, not nominally free
  products.
- Form a minimum access compact with mutual recognition and portable eligibility.

### International institutions

- Define comparable access measures and audit rules.
- Pool procurement where national buying power is weak.
- Finance transition capacity in countries that cannot pre-position it alone.
- Monitor cross-border variance as an early-warning signal in its own right.

## 🧪 What would falsify this proposal

A serious transition plan should say what evidence would change it.

- If falling prices reliably lift access margins for exposed cohorts without
  intervention, the case for heavy automatic stabilisers weakens.
- If permission differences shrink as capability spreads, the migration of the
  binding if is overstated.
- If essential human work remains well staffed while basic needs become cheaper,
  the labour paradox is smaller than forecast.
- If zero-cost services preserve quality, privacy, autonomy and broad eligibility
  without public purchasing or access obligations, the institutional proposal is
  too state-heavy.
- If calendar phases predict turning points better than the proposed gates, keep
  the calendar. Until then, gates are the more honest planning device.

## ❓ Decisions still belonging to Fer

  access to a defined floor?
- Is permission the expected final constraint, or one hypothesis the dashboard
  should test alongside atoms, energy, ownership and geography?

- Which protections should activate automatically, and which require a fresh
  democratic decision?
