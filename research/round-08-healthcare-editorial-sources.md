# Healthcare sources for the first public piece

The first piece demonstrates questions about access using US healthcare.
It contains one statistical figure and a short financing contrast. These
source checks support those narrow statements, not an individual diagnosis.
The earlier [healthcare review](2026-09-09-healthcare-if-validation.md) retains
its broader comparison and limitations.

Sources checked on 2026-09-09:

| Source | Statement used | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [US Census Bureau, Health Insurance Coverage in the United States: 2024, 2025-09-09](https://www.census.gov/library/publications/2025/demo/p60-288.html) | 92.0% had insurance for some or all of 2024, from CPS ASEC | Coverage is neither visit affordability nor delivered access; use as historical observation |
| [HealthCare.gov deductible glossary](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/deductible/) | Covered care may involve deductibles and other cost sharing; some care is covered before the deductible | Plan and service determine terms; no individual legal or clinical advice |
| [HHS licensing across state lines](https://telehealth.hhs.gov/licensure/licensing-across-state-lines) | Several licensing routes exist; profession and state matter | The article does not adjudicate a particular clinician's authority |
| [Healthy People 2030 health literacy](https://odphp.health.gov/healthypeople/priority-areas/health-literacy-healthy-people-2030) | Personal and organisational health literacy are distinct parts of using information and services | Definition, not measured prevalence or proof of a binding barrier; page carries a notice of restoration to its January 2025 version |
| [Spanish Ministry of Health common service portfolio](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/profesionales/prestacionesSanitarias/CarteraDeServicios/) | Common basic portfolio financed publicly | Supplementary services and eligibility require separate treatment |
| [Belgium federal portal healthcare costs](https://www.belgium.be/en/health/healthcare_costs) | Official rates and reimbursement arrangements | Financing mechanics, not zero patient burden or an observed access rate |
| [Australian Department of Health Medicare costs](https://www.health.gov.au/topics/medicare/about/costs) | Bulk billing and gap-paying services coexist | Service financing mechanism, not a patient's appointment availability |

The previously used Belgian FPS URL returned an error during this check.
The federal portal above supplies the narrower financing statement. HHS's
licensing page uses `/licensure/`, not the earlier `/providers/licensure/`
path. The proximity and availability passages are questions in an explicitly
imagined care journey, without prevalence claims. There is no assertion that
AI diagnosis is solved, safe, free or authorised.
