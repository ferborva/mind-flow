# Income-access pilot feasibility

## 🦅 TL;DR

**The promising unlock is a transition laboratory, not a premature storm detector.** Three official longitudinal programmes offer ways to examine the same people over time. None of the inspected metadata establishes the full set of income-generating routes available to a person, current national disruption, or useful warning lead time.

**Recommend a conditional U.S. SIPP historical-method benchmark first, while keeping the live pilot country undecided.** Its monthly histories and public-use route make it a practical place to test the distinction between employment loss, income loss and access loss. HILDA remains the conditional Australian-depth option. Understanding Society supplies an especially useful challenge: its own documentation describes apparent occupation changes caused partly by measurement changes, not social change. [S6][S9][S13]

This recommendation is Ren's commissioned research judgement. It is not Fernando's settled choice of country, severity, duration, household treatment, alternative-route standard or interpretation of the 5% threshold. The [machine-readable proposal](../pilots/income-access/feasibility.v1.json) keeps those decisions unresolved. No individual records were acquired, no participant contacted, no provider permission sought, and no measurement admitted.

## 🔬 The decision we can make now

The first decision is whether a candidate can support a **bounded falsifiable measurement exercise**. It is not whether the dataset is prestigious, large or conveniently downloadable. The candidate must make us more able to distinguish competing explanations for a change.

The intended chain is:

> Observed transition → income consequence → feasible alternatives → changed IF → possible preparation.

An analyst may get through the first two links and have no evidence for the third. That is a valuable diagnosis of the gap, not permission to label the whole chain measured.

The scope is deliberately asymmetric. We can compare data structures now. We cannot choose a valid final cohort, estimate its precision, settle a national affected-share estimand or claim data-user eligibility from metadata alone. Those require the subsequent gates below.

## 🧭 Shortlist and selection logic

The comparison uses qualitative judgements, not a weighted score with arbitrary decimal precision. Access friction, timing and construct fit can trade off; no sum makes a missing construct acceptable.

| Route | Reason to investigate | Main reason not to overinvest yet | Proposed role |
|---|---|---|---|
| U.S. SIPP | Monthly income and employment histories [S6] | Release lag; realised outcomes do not reveal every feasible option | Historical-method benchmark |
| Australian HILDA | Income, employment, household and health context [S1] | Access permission and a question-by-wave crosswalk still needed | Conditional Australian pathway study |
| UK Understanding Society | Multi-job detail and documented measurement changes [S13] | Licensing and cross-wave interpretation require care | Alternatives and construct-change comparator |

**This is a deliberately narrow feasibility shortlist, not a worldwide ranking.** All three are from wealthy countries with established survey infrastructure. Success would not establish transportability to economies with different informal work, household sharing, survey coverage or institutional arrangements. A later country expansion needs a fresh construct and access review, not merely translated labels.

### U.S. SIPP

**Fact.** The inspected 2025 release covers January to December 2024 and was released on 15 July 2026. That establishes a material gap between the observed period and availability; “monthly histories” must not be displayed as “monthly live updates.” [S7][S8]

**Fact.** The guide defines a civilian noninstitutionalised universe and provides person-month linkage, employment/earnings, imputation, survey-weight and variance guidance. [S9]

**Judgement.** A historical cohort could test whether an employment transition is followed by an earnings interruption and whether other observed income changes coincide. The method must not equate another income receipt with an acceptable alternative route. It also must not count every job ending as involuntary loss.

**Access.** A public-use route is identified, not executed. Census policy qualifies its general U.S. copyright statement for foreign jurisdictions and nonemployee works. Public availability does not settle this project's permitted processing, redistribution or disclosure practice. The review gate therefore remains open. [S9][S11]

**Continuity risk.** A Federal Register notice describes proposed changes to SIPP collection mode, sampling, weighting and reference period. This is evidence of planned testing, not proof of cancellation or completed comparability. Any future series must pass a fresh bridge assessment. [S10]

### Australian HILDA

**Fact.** Release 24 is the cumulative release listed for researcher applications. Wave 24 fieldwork extended from July 2024 into March 2025. The manual requires an application and confidentiality deed; the inspected access page does not establish this project's approval. [S2][S3]

**Judgement.** HILDA is attractive for investigating how a change in income relates to other access conditions in Australia. The existing Australian evidence room creates useful context, but its regional employment aggregates do not become linked respondent histories simply because the country matches.

**Measurement risk.** Analysis-specific longitudinal weights, nonresponse and attrition matter. Selecting only persistent responders can alter the population being described. Household-panel exclusions must be declared rather than silently using the whole country's population as the denominator. [S3][S4]

**Access risk.** Obtain current terms, applicant eligibility and permitted processing-environment rules before applying or downloading. A questionnaire for a newer wave is documentation, not a newly available measurement. [S2][S5]

### UK Understanding Society

**Fact.** The inspected catalogue lists Waves 1-15 through 2024 and an April 2026 edition. Registered-user conditions apply; commercial use requires approval. More detailed access tiers carry additional restrictions. [S12][S14]

**Fact.** Annual interviews and multi-job detail are promising, but wave boundaries overlap calendar years. The guide describes revised occupation coding after apparently elevated job changes, demonstrating why an instrument change must not automatically become a storm. [S13]

**Judgement.** This is an excellent adversarial comparator for the IF-evolution mechanism. It is not the lowest-friction first benchmark, and the commercial weather-station metaphor does not determine the actual legal use classification. Resolve the intended project use with the accountable custodian before selecting an access tier.

## 🧩 Define disruption without smuggling in a threshold

The proposed construct is **loss or material degradation of a feasible, adequate and acceptable income-generating route**, subject to explicit population, duration and alternative-route choices. Every adjective is an unresolved measurement requirement, not a measured fact.

Separate these observations:

| Observation | What it may tell us | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| A job ended | A realised employment arrangement changed | Whether departure was voluntary or income access was lost |
| Earnings fell | A recorded flow changed | Whether viable alternatives existed or were refused |
| Benefits or household income rose | A possible income buffer changed | Whether the person controlled or could rely on that resource |
| Job-search activity stopped | A reported behaviour changed | Whether a person became discouraged, found an alternative, became unable to search or chose not to |
| Training began | A preparation activity occurred | Whether it was affordable, completed or led to acceptable work |
| Several barriers were reported | Multiple conditions may matter | Which one was binding, whether they were simultaneous or causally sufficient |

**Alternatives should be observed as alternatives, not inferred from imagination.** A vacancy is not an offer; an offer is not feasible if unaffordable transport or care makes it unusable; a nominal permission is not actual access. The initial pilot can expose where such facts are absent. It cannot resolve them by applying a national average.

For each route, use the existing dimensions as questions: price, permission, proximity, availability and capability. Record whether each has evidence, is out of scope, is contested or is simply unknown. Keep multiple plausible blockers when the data cannot distinguish them.

## 🧮 A bounded first estimand

**Proposed research question:** among a predeclared cohort observed with employment or self-employment at baseline, what transitions in observed work and earnings occur during a specified later interval, and which alternative income flows are observed?

This is narrower than “who lost income-generating access.” It is deliberately named differently. The question can be rejected if relevant variables, reasons or time alignment do not support it.

The analysis specification should freeze:

1. Cohort entry, observation window, inclusions, exclusions and linkage rules.
2. Whether the denominator is baseline cohort membership or another explicitly defined population. Complete cases are not an automatic denominator.
3. Separate labels for involuntary change, voluntary change, ambiguity and missing information, only where supported by instrument wording.
4. Earnings components and periods, observed buffers, imputation flags and incompatible reference clocks.
5. Correct weights, design-based uncertainty, attrition sensitivity and disclosure restrictions.
6. A primary result and an explicit failure result before inspecting outcomes.

No severity or persistence number is chosen here. The approved analysis may study several clearly named transitions without calling any of them the national storm definition. Threshold sensitivity is not a substitute for deciding which construct matters.

**Never multiply a pilot percentage by the country's population unless the sampling and estimand justify that inference.** “Five per cent of baseline-employed respondents with usable records” and “five per cent of people in the country newly disrupted” are different quantities. Household exposure requires another estimand, de-duplication and explicit treatment of resource sharing.

## ⚖️ Challenge the recommendation

**The strongest case against SIPP is conceptual, not computational:** its accessibility could reward us for measuring the wrong thing quickly. An accurate earnings-transition estimate would still miss feasible routes never taken, unacceptable alternatives, informal support or the personal agency associated with a household resource.

The proposed countermeasure is not to add caveats after producing a dramatic chart. It is to freeze the narrower label before analysis and make a missing-access result a valid outcome. If the benchmark cannot distinguish a required transition, stop that estimand. Do not replace it with unemployment and preserve the stronger title.

**The strongest case against an Australia-first rule is convenience bias.** Existing local assets reduce engineering friction, but they do not supply individual linkage or provider permission. Australia becomes the live pilot only if its measurement and operational gates justify that choice.

**The strongest case against our panel-first shortlist is speed.** Historical panels may teach mechanisms yet arrive too late for preparation. A usable warning service may need a separately validated timely sentinel linked to later outcome measurement. These are two roles with separate clocks, not interchangeable datasets. No sentinel is selected or forecast issued here.

Role names are requirements, not appointments. Neither a repository commit nor passing tests changes these statuses. Participant research, spending, provider contact and public release each remain separately controlled.

## ✅ Deliverable and next action

The shipped research artefact is a **versioned, machine-checked feasibility proposal**, not a validated dataset or source authentication system. Its summary is suitable for the station's “What would unlock measurement?” surface. It records the three options, the conditional benchmark recommendation and the exact remaining decisions.

The next valuable step is the release-specific variable crosswalk described in the [pilot protocol](../pilots/income-access/measurement-protocol.md), followed by independent construct review. Only then should an accountable human authorise the smallest acquisition. If that gate fails, the station should show a precise missing measurement rather than a fabricated warning.
