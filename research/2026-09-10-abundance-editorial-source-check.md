# Sources for the restored abundance illustrations

The restored examples separate Fernando's captured argument from external
claims. The older [figures note](2026-09-07-abundance-figures.md) remains the
record of the original research; its recommendation that a global poverty
distribution identifies the abundance audience is not supported by the data.

## The distribution table

The retained [9 September snapshot](../dashboard/snapshots/2026-09-09.r1.json)
contains World 2026 values 44.3844 for `poverty-830` and 80.0368 for `poverty-30`.
Rounding to one decimal gives 44.4% and 80.0%. These are inherited macro
nowcasts, not observations newly collected in Round 08. The whole-snapshot
raw-input status is `not_pinned`; the source response needed to replay those
historical values independently is missing. This limitation stays beside the
table in the essay, rather than being hidden behind the source link.

The OWID indicator pages were checked on 2026-09-10:

- [Share below $8.30/day](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-living-with-less-than-upper-middle-income-poverty-line).
- [Share below $30/day](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/poverty-share-on-less-than-30-per-day).

Their metadata describes World Bank PIP inputs, 2021 purchasing-power-parity
units, mixed household income/consumption survey concepts, and global estimates
extended using national-accounts growth estimates or forecasts. The pages name
June 26, 2026 as their data update. They do not identify cash holdings, service
prices, individual eligibility or the audience for abundance rhetoric.

The documented CSV requests returned HTTP 403 during this recheck, including
the full-data URLs listed by the publisher. Therefore this note verifies the
retained snapshot values and current methodological description, not a fresh
upstream reproduction of the numerical nowcast. The table must not be labelled
newly measured, direct survey prevalence in 2026 or an independently replayed
source result. Recovering the original raw response remains open.

## Musk: report the optimism and its conditions

The [World Economic Forum transcript](https://www.weforum.org/podcasts/meet-the-leader/episodes/conversation-with-elon-musk-davos-2026/)
records the January 22, 2026 conversation with Larry Fink. Musk's optimistic
scenario includes ubiquitous low-cost AI and robotics, and he explicitly names
electrical power as a deployment constraint. The essay paraphrases those
positions, not the truth of the prediction. This primary source defeats an
absolute claim that he never names conditions.

[Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/musk-predicts-ai-create-universal-high-income-make-saving-money-unnecessary)
reports Musk's May 2024 Viva Technology wording “universal high income”. The
essay uses only that short attributed phrase. The longer earlier quotations,
money-by-2036 claim and loose recollections about other podcast speakers are
not restored. No quotation comes from an agent role-playing a named person.

## Country illustrations

- [Belgium's official healthcare-costs guide](https://www.belgium.be/en/health/healthcare_costs),
  checked 2026-09-10, describes compulsory insurance and reimbursement. It does
  not support a blanket assertion that patients ultimately pay nothing.
- [Spain's national service portfolio](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/profesionales/prestacionesSanitarias/CarteraDeServicios/),
  checked 2026-09-10, distinguishes the publicly financed basic portfolio from
  supplementary services involving contributions. It does not support calling
  every healthcare item universally free or immediately available.
- [Guardia Civil arms-control information](https://web.guardiacivil.es/es/tramites/autorizaciones-de-armas-y-explosivos/controldearmas/),
  checked 2026-09-10, supplies the narrow licensing example. The essay gives no
  possession, acquisition or use instructions and makes no individual
  eligibility determination.

These sources correct the structural details around captured illustrations;
they do not establish Fernando's preference for one healthcare system. His
September 9 stance explicitly leaves that solution open.
