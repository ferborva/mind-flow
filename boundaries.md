# Evidence and authority boundaries

The Observatory offers research and a way to examine conditional promises.
Fernando's contribution is the frame and method. Ren's commissioned proposals
remain proposals until Fernando adopts them. Public writing stays in review;
Fernando decides what to publish.

Agency is the goal and remains unmeasured. Signals observe specified
conditions for a named population, service, place and time. A national survey
does not diagnose a particular person's barrier. A matched geography does not
establish that the same people appear in two datasets. Rule eligibility does
not show delivered service, and a modelled employment series does not identify
why employment changed.

Every number needs retained bytes, the source's vintage, units, population and
a reproducible derivation. Source hashes support integrity checks, not source
truth. Stale, missing and conflicting observations remain distinct. A
calculated condition state does not establish that the condition is causally
binding, that the condition set is complete, or that an intervention works.
Evidence ceilings belong beside their claims even when general disclaimers
are moved here.

The instruments create no clinical advice, warning, negotiation, action,
recruitment or publication authority. A condition owner named in a research
record has not thereby accepted a duty, funded a service or consented to a
trial. Operational decisions require the relevant lawful and accountable
human process. The existing human-protocol stop-lines remain in force.

Forecasts are issued only through their defined prospective process. A
scenario is not a schedule, a baseline fit is not calibration and a score is
withheld until the corresponding outcome can be validly resolved. Records
preserve misses and corrections as well as successes.

Agent creator receipts are claims. Independent reviewers must reproduce
results from the frozen tree and retained inputs, challenge the inferential
steps, and report unmet gates. Agreement between agents is not evidence.
