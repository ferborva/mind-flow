                     <section id="section-9033-5-9033" class="row ps-section section-no-title">
                        <div id="9033-5" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h1 class=" h1" id="toc-5-1" data-prefix="">5. The Safety Net Scheme</h1>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-45" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12"></div>
                        <div id="9033-44" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <p>The PBS Safety Net protects patients and their families requiring a large number of
                              PBS, RPBS or non-PBS public outpatient items. For the purposes of the scheme, the
                              family includes:</p>
                           <ul>
                              <li>the person</li>
                              <li>the person’s partner or de facto partner</li>
                              <li>children under the age of 16 who are in the care and control of the person</li>
                              <li>full-time students under the age of 25 who are dependent on the person.</li>
                           </ul>
                           <p>The scheme requires pharmacists, approved medical practitioners, or approved hospital
                              authorities, on request by patients, to record the supply of PBS, RPBS, and non-PBS
                              medicines you get from the outpatient pharmacy of a public hospital on a PBS/RPBS
                              Safety Net prescription record form and application for a Safety Net card form (<a href="https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/pb240" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">PB240</a>). Alternatively, a Prescription Record Form can be generated from the approved suppliers
                              dispensing software on request. When a patient exactly meets or exceeds the Safety
                              Net threshold within a calendar year, they qualify to receive PBS or RPBS items at
                              a lower price or free of charge for the rest of that calendar year. Any applicable
                              special patient contributions, brand premiums or therapeutic group premiums must still
                              be met by the patient.</p>
                           <p>The Safety Net threshold is reached by accumulating eligible patient contributions
                              for PBS prescriptions supplied through community pharmacies, private hospitals, and
                              PBS/RPBS and non-PBS medicines you get from the outpatient pharmacy of a public hospital.</p>
                           <p>PBS/RPBS benefits (including authority items) can only be counted towards the Safety
                              Net threshold when prescribed and supplied according to PBS conditions. A medicine
                              supplied by a community pharmacist not approved to supply PBS benefits cannot count
                              towards the Safety Net.</p>
                           <p>Prescriptions for some PBS items are not eligible for Safety Net benefits if re‑supplied
                              as an ‘early supply’ (i.e. within a specified period after a previous supply of the
                              same or an equivalent item for the same person. The patient contribution for an early
                              supply does not count towards the Safety Net threshold (see also details under&nbsp;<a href="Section_1_4_Explanatory_Notes" class="xref">4. Patient Charges</a>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<a href="Section_1_7_Explanatory_Notes" class="xref">7. How Pharmacists Claim Reimbursement</a>'). Early supply arrangements do not apply to any prescriptions originating from a
                              hospital (including outpatient medications).</p>
                           <p>Some states and territories have Pharmaceutical Reform Agreements (PRA) with the Commonwealth
                              to support access to PBS medicines in certain public hospital settings. The PRAs enable
                              approved public hospitals to prescribe and dispense PBS-subsidised medicines and chemotherapy
                              drugs to day-admitted patients, non-admitted patients, and admitted patients on discharge.
                              These agreements are in place between the Commonwealth and all jurisdictions except
                              New South Wales and the Australian Capital Territory. Access to PBS medicines under
                              the Section 100 Highly Specialised (HSD) Program is not reliant on a PRA being in
                              place as these medicines are available under alternative arrangements made under Section
                              100 of the National Health Act 1953.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-42" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-42-1" data-prefix="">Safety Net thresholds</h2>
                           <p>There are two Safety Net thresholds. From 1 January 2026 the general patient Safety
                              Net threshold is $1,748.20.&nbsp;When a person’s and/or their family's total eligible payments
                              for PBS and non-PBS public hospital outpatient prescriptions exactly meet or exceed
                              this amount, they may apply for a Safety Net concession card and pay the concessional
                              co-payment amount of $7.70 plus any applicable premium for PBS benefits for the rest
                              of that calendar year.</p>
                           <p>From 1 January 2026 the concessional Safety Net threshold remains at $277.20 (this
                              also applies to DVA Gold, White or Orange card holders under the RPBS). When a person’s
                              and/or their family's total eligible payments exactly meet or exceed this amount,
                              they may apply for a Safety Net entitlement card and may receive pharmaceutical benefits
                              free of charge (except for any applicable premium) for the rest of that calendar year.</p>
                           <p>Brand premiums, therapeutic group premiums and special patient contributions do not
                              count towards the Safety Net thresholds.</p>
                           <p>The Safety Net thresholds are normally adjusted on 1 January each year in line with
                              movements in the Consumer Price Index (CPI). The concessional Safety Net threshold
                              will not be indexed in 2026.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-43" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h3 class=" h3" id="toc-43-1" data-prefix="">Safety Net cross-over arrangements</h3>
                           <p>Some patients and/or members of their families will change between general patient
                              and concessional patient status during a calendar year.</p>
                           <p>Concessional patients who were previously general patients can apply for a Safety
                              Net entitlement card when they exactly meet or exceed the concessional Safety Net
                              threshold. In this case, any pharmaceutical benefits previously supplied at the general
                              co-payment rate in that calendar year will be counted at the concessional rate per
                              item.</p>
                           <p><b>General patients</b>&nbsp;who were previously concessional patients can apply for a Safety Net entitlement
                              card when they exactly meet or exceed the concessional Safety Net threshold. In this
                              case, any PBS and RPBS benefits previously supplied at the general rate in that calendar
                              year will be counted at the concessional rate per item.</p>
                           <p>They can also choose to apply for a PBS Safety Net concession card when they exactly
                              meet or exceed the general PBS Safety Net threshold. In this case, all PBS medicines
                              will count at the patient contribution rate paid for the medicine.</p>
                           <p>In the case of families where one parent holds a concession card and other family
                              members are general patients, the family can choose to apply for either a Safety Net
                              entitlement card or a Safety Net concession card.</p>
                           <p>To receive a Safety Net entitlement card, all PBS and RPBS benefits (including general
                              pharmaceutical benefits) are counted at the concessional rate per item until the concessional
                              threshold is reached.</p>
                           <p>White DVA card holders may either be general or concessional patients (depending on
                              their Centrelink entitlements). If they are receiving treatment for a specific disability
                              accepted by the DVA, they are also supplied with specified items under the RPBS at
                              the concessional rate per item. Therefore, these patients are encouraged to maintain
                              a concessional prescription record form, plus a general prescription record form for
                              items not covered under the RPBS.</p>
                           <p>White card holders may choose at any time to count contributions made at the general
                              level towards the concessional Safety Net threshold and receive credits equal to the
                              concessional co-payment amount for each pharmaceutical benefit purchased. Alternatively,
                              white card holders can count contributions at the concessional level towards the general
                              Safety Net, and receive credits equal to the concessional co-payment amount for each
                              pharmaceutical benefit purchased.</p>
                           <p>Gold or orange DVA card holders may receive all of their prescription items under
                              the RPBS, and only pay the concessional co-payment amount for each item.</p>
                           <p>Dependants of white, gold or orange card holders are treated separately and may be
                              either general patients or concessional patients. Their prescriptions may be included
                              in the cross-over arrangements.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-41" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-41-1" data-prefix="">Recording PBS prescriptions</h2>
                           <p>From 2018 the PBS/RPBS Safety Net prescription record form and application for a Safety
                              Net card form (PB240) replaced the PBS Prescription Record Form (PB015) and the PBS
                              Hospital Prescription Record Form (PB016).</p>
                           <p>The form can be downloaded and printed by patients or approved suppliers by going
                              to&nbsp;<a href="https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/pb240" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">PBS/RPBS Safety Net prescription record form and application for a Safety Net card
                                 form (PB240) - Services Australia</a>. Alternatively, a Prescription Record Form can be generated from the approved suppliers
                              dispensing software on request.</p>
                           <p>Although the former PB015 and PB016 forms are no longer produced, some pharmacies
                              may have a stock of them and they can still be used to record patient PBS expenditure.</p>
                           <p>The PB240 form is used by the patient/family to track their PBS Safety Net expenditure.
                              They keep the form and provide it to the pharmacist each time they have:</p>
                           <ul>
                              <li>PBS medicines dispensed at any PBS approved supplier, including community pharmacies,
                                 approved medical practitioners, private hospitals and public hospitals; and</li>
                              <li>Non-PBS medicines you get from the outpatient pharmacy of a public hospital.</li>
                           </ul>
                           <p>The pharmacist records the medicine by placing the label printed by their software
                              or by handwriting the information onto the form. If a patient/family use multiple
                              pharmacies, it is recommended they keep track of their expenditure using this form.</p>
                           <p>Patients should record their general or concessional status on the prescription record
                              form, enter their Centrelink, DVA and/or Safety Net Concession/Entitlement Card number,
                              and list family members covered. General patients must also record their Medicare
                              number when applying for a Safety Net concession card.</p>
                           <p>Details to be entered on the prescription record form by the pharmacist are:</p>
                           <ul>
                              <li>date of supply</li>
                              <li>PBS/RPBS code number of the item (for community pharmacies only)</li>
                              <li>the Safety Net value of the item (for community pharmacies only)</li>
                              <li>pharmacist's approval number (for community pharmacies only)</li>
                              <li>item identification — medicine code, name of medicine or abbreviation (for public
                                 hospitals only)</li>
                              <li>hospital approval number (for private and reform public hospitals only)</li>
                              <li>approved medical practitioners approval number (for approved medical practitioners
                                 only)</li>
                              <li>hospital charge (for public hospitals only)</li>
                              <li>hospital Safety Net number (for non-reform public hospitals only)</li>
                              <li>signature of the authorised person making the entry.</li>
                           </ul>
                           <p>Community pharmacists, approved medical practitioners, private hospitals, and public
                              hospitals dispensing PBS items should record in the 'Safety Net value' column:</p>
                           <ul>
                              <li>the patient contribution when it is less than the PBS dispensed price</li>
                              <li>the Safety Net value shown in the Schedule, or any lesser amount charged, if the PBS
                                 dispensed price is less than or equal to the patient contribution. The pharmacist
                                 may discount the price for these items.</li>
                           </ul>
                           <p>Public hospital pharmacies dispensing non-PBS items to out-patients should record
                              in the 'Safety Net value' column:</p>
                           <ul>
                              <li>hospital charge for non-PBS medicines a patient receives from the outpatient pharmacy
                                 of a public hospital.</li>
                           </ul>
                           <p>Some computer software suppliers provide a special label to record this information
                              on the prescription record forms. Some suppliers also provide a computer printout
                              as a prescription record form.</p>
                           <p>The patient is responsible for maintenance and storage of their prescription record
                              form. However, it may be kept in the pharmacy. A person (or family) may have more
                              than one prescription record form.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-39" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-39-1" data-prefix="">Hospital prescription record forms</h2>
                           <p>Items to be recorded on hospital prescription record forms must be approved by the
                              hospital's pharmaceutical advisory committee and may be listed on a hospital's formulary
                              (a list of pharmaceutical items approved by the committee for the treatment of particular
                              illnesses), or authorised on a patient-by-patient basis.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-38" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-38-1" data-prefix="">Multi-item prescription forms</h2>
                           <p>If a patient submits a multi-item PBS prescription form, which would take the total
                              co-payments equal to or past the Safety Net threshold, any items in excess are treated
                              as entitled items once a Safety Net entitlement/concession card is issued.</p>
                           <p>Excess items should be treated as 'deferred supply' items.</p>
                           <p>For example, if a family has a new PBS prescription for three items and the first
                              takes the family to less than one co-payment under the threshold, this item should
                              be supplied at the general rate. If the second item takes the family exactly to or
                              over the threshold, the pharmacist should then issue a Safety Net concession card
                              and supply both this and the third item at the concessional rate. This involves the
                              deferral of two items, recording the Safety Net concession card number, and the subsequent
                              supply of these items.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-37" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-37-1" data-prefix="">Qualifying PBS prescriptions</h2>
                           <p>A PBS prescription should be supplied at the concessional rate or free of charge plus
                              any applicable premium, when the Safety Net value or hospital charge for that PBS
                              prescription takes the total eligible payments equal to or over the qualifying Safety
                              Net threshold amount for a Safety Net entitlement/concession card.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-36" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-36-1" data-prefix="">Lost prescription record forms</h2>
                           <p>If a prescription record form has been lost, stolen or destroyed, a pharmacist may
                              prepare a duplicate copy, but is under no obligation to do so.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-35" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-35-1" data-prefix="">Retrospective entitlement and patient refunds</h2>
                           <p>Responsibility for claiming entitlements rests with the patient. If items recorded
                              on a prescription record form have exceeded the Safety Net threshold, the cost of
                              those items in excess of the limit cannot be refunded by a pharmacist.</p>
                           <p>However, if the patient failed to apply for a Safety Net entitlement/concession card
                              on reaching the Safety Net threshold they should write to Services Australia and provide
                              copies of pharmacy accounts or a signed statement from the pharmacist giving the date
                              of supply, description and cost of items supplied and paid for. A copy of the relevant
                              prescription record form should also be provided. If these are not available, the
                              patient should give the name of the pharmacy where the card was issued and the number
                              on the card so that Services Australia can locate the prescription record form in
                              its records. Cash refunds are not available.&nbsp; Services Australia contact details are
                              provided in the 'Addresses — Services Australia' part of the Schedule.</p>
                           <p>If the patient cannot satisfy a pharmacist that they have a current entitlement and
                              is charged the general patient price, the pharmacist should issue the patient with
                              a receipt and a claim form (provided by Services Australia). The patient can then
                              obtain a refund via Medicare Service Centres or PBS processing centres. RPBS prescription
                              refunds are paid at DVA State offices.</p>
                           <p>Services Australia can only pay refunds for PBS items supplied through approved pharmacies.
                              Refunds for hospital supplied items should be referred to the relevant hospital or
                              health department. Refunds cannot be made where the patient was charged the general
                              or concessional amount instead of the Safety Net concessional or Safety Net entitlement
                              amount as a result of the Safety Net early supply rule. Receipts for prescriptions
                              where the Safety Net early supply rule has applied must include ‘SNESR’ to indicate
                              the reason for the amount charged.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-34" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-34-1" data-prefix="">Applying for a Safety Net Entitlement/Concession Card</h2>
                           <p>Once the Safety Net threshold has been exactly reached or exceeded, the person covered
                              by a prescription record form may complete the application and declaration to get
                              a Safety Net entitlement/concession card. Please note that software packages that
                              produce computer generated applications must be approved by Services Australia.</p>
                           <p>If the card is issued to a dependent child or student, it should be in the name of
                              a parent.</p>
                           <p>When issuing entitlement/concession cards, pharmacists do not have to check all prescription
                              record form details. However, they should ensure each entry has been signed and that
                              the prescription record form total qualifies the patient for the relevant Safety Net
                              card.</p>
                           <p>When appropriate the pharmacist should check that the patient's Medicare card number
                              is on the prescription record form.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-33" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-33-1" data-prefix="">Issuing a Safety Net Entitlement/Concession Card</h2>
                           <p>When satisfied that the individual or family is entitled, the pharmacist should issue
                              the next blank Safety Net entitlement/concession card with the following details:</p>
                           <ul>
                              <li>the names of family members covered. If there are more than eight family members,
                                 a second card should be issued listing the card holder and family members not listed
                                 on the first card. The prescription record form has space to record that two cards
                                 have been issued, and</li>
                              <li>the two-character code to indicate the relationship to the card holder. Applicable
                                 codes are:
                                 
                                 </li>
                              <ul>
                                 <li>SP - partner</li>
                                 <li>DC - child under 16 years</li>
                                 <li>DS - dependent full-time student under 25 years.</li>
                              </ul>
                           </ul>
                           <p>The pharmacist should be satisfied that only family members are listed on the card.
                              The unused space on the card should be ruled through to prevent extra names being
                              added. The sticky label from the Safety Net entitlement/concession card, pre-printed
                              with the card number, should be attached to the prescription record form. The pharmacist
                              should sign and stamp each prescription record form with the pharmacy stamp and enter
                              the card issue details on a Safety Net — claim for payment form.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-32" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-32-1" data-prefix="">Issuing supplementary cards</h2>
                           <p>A pharmacist may give a card holder a supplementary card for a partner or dependant
                              only at the time the original card is issued. The duplicate card should be recorded
                              in the additional box on the prescription record form.</p>
                           <p>Later requests for supplementary cards and requests to add a new family member to
                              the original card are to be referred to Services Australia.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-31" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-31-1" data-prefix="">Notification to Services Australia and claim for payment</h2>
                           <p>Payment for issuing a Safety Net entitlement/concession card is made after the Safety
                              Net — claim for payment form is sent to Services Australia, no later than one month
                              after a card is issued.</p>
                           <p>Each form must be accompanied by all supporting documentation (prescription record
                              form and cancelled or void Safety Net entitlement/concession cards).</p>
                           <p>Payment will not be made for void cards.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-30" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-30-1" data-prefix="">Lost Safety Net Entitlement/Concession Cards</h2>
                           <p>When a card has been lost, damaged, stolen or destroyed, a pharmacist cannot re-issue
                              a person with a replacement card. The original card holder (or partner) must apply
                              to Services Australia.</p>
                        </div>
                        <div id="9033-29" class="psml-fragment col-xs-12">
                           <h2 class=" h2" id="toc-29-1" data-prefix="">Pharmacy record of issued cards</h2>
                           <p>A record of all cards issued must be kept at the pharmacy from which the pharmacist
                              is approved to supply pharmaceutical benefits. The duplicate ('bookfast') copy in
                              the Safety Net — claim for payment book is provided for this purpose.</p>
                        </div>
