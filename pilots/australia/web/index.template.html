Competing explanations

What else could move this line?

None of these is a decorative caveat. A causal claim must separate them with an appropriate design.

Local demand

Business cycles, industry mix and employer demand can change employment without AI deployment.

Population

Migration, commuting and residence changes can alter a place-of-residence series.

Work location

The estimate follows where people live, not where the employing business operates.

Substitution

Outsourcing, offshoring and non-generative software may alter the occupation independently.

Classification

Occupation recoding and the ANZSCO to OSCA transition can create artificial breaks.

The model itself

Smoothing, revisions and patterns learned from history can miss novel structural change.
