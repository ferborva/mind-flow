# Round 02 synthesis and Round 03 plan

> **This is Ren's synthesis of three immutable agent reviews, not Fernando's
> adopted position and not an approval. Public release and operational use
> remain blocked.** The original findings remain intact in `reviews/external/`.
> The machine-readable disposition is in
> `reviews/round-02-disposition-ledger.json`.

## 🦅 TL;DR

**The goal is clear, but the product identity must narrow.** This project
should not become an oracle that announces the arrival of abundance. It should
be public decision infrastructure for answering five harder questions:

1. For whom has a named capability become real access and freely chosen agency?
2. Which conditions still prevent that crossing, and what evidence would show
   that they changed?
3. Which adverse transition paths are becoming more plausible, without
   pretending that coverage is probability or association is cause?
4. Which preparations remain useful across several futures, and which actions
   become legitimate only after explicit evidence, authority and consent?
5. Did action improve lived outcomes without shifting labour, household,
   institutional or ecological harm elsewhere?

The central mechanism is not a dashboard. It is a versioned, challengeable IF
record linking a scoped promise to evidence, decision rights, action and
experienced outcomes. The public experience is valuable only if it improves
understanding and decisions over a simpler official-statistics release and
deliberation process.

Round 02 proved that the repository can preserve a deterministic review
artifact and can refuse to call unknown conditions true. It also exposed ten
stop-lines. Most importantly, the current system can manufacture release
authority from self-attested text, can invert the public meaning of `UNLESS`,
can publish transformed numbers without the exact upstream evidence chain, and
can accept data structures that create false or unsafe displays.

**Round 03 should therefore be an integrity round, not a feature round.** No
additional visual ambition should outrun the truth, authority and harm
boundaries underneath it.

## 🎯 The goal, made precise

### The mission

> Help people, communities and legitimate institutions recognise when
> technological capability is, or is not, becoming shared and durable human
> agency, then prepare and act early without manufacturing certainty,
> authority or consent.

This is a normative mission. It is not an empirical result and should not be
presented as one.

### The research question

> Under which observable conditions does actual adoption of a named capability
> improve specified outcomes and voluntary choice for a named population,
> place and period, without transferring material labour, household,
> institutional or ecological harm?

This can generate bounded hypotheses that may lose.

### The instrument question

> Does a public, provenance-rich condition map improve comprehension, error
> correction, decision quality and appropriate early preparation compared
> with a simpler official-statistics release and the same deliberative support,
> without increasing anxiety, false authority, stigma, surveillance or
> self-fulfilling behaviour?

The default should be the simpler instrument until evidence shows that the
Observatory adds material value within affected-party-approved harm bounds.

### The product's legitimate role

The Observatory may become:

- a provenance and correction layer attached to trusted statistics;
- a conditional foresight tool that preserves uncertainty and alternatives;
- a shared memory of forecasts, decisions, dissent, outcomes and errors;
- a place where directly affected people can change definitions, thresholds
  and permitted actions;
- a federation protocol for comparable evidence without erasing local or
  Indigenous authority.

It must not claim to be:

- a central predictor of an inevitable social future;
- an authority to declare a crisis or direct action;
- a substitute for official statistics, democratic institutions or community
  governance;
- proof that technological capability naturally becomes shared abundance;
- an elegant interface whose coherence is mistaken for empirical truth.

**The Hari Seldon inspiration should be mathematical memory and disciplined
foresight, not omniscience.** The strongest Foundation-like institution is one
that remembers its assumptions, scores its forecasts, preserves dissent and
changes course visibly when reality wins.

## ⚖️ How I would debate the position

### What I would agree with

1. **Capability is not wellbeing.** A system can become technically able to
   produce more while access, security, autonomy or distribution worsen.
2. **The crossing is conditional.** Price, ownership, infrastructure,
   eligibility, delivery, care, privacy, power, ecology and institutions can
   each bind after technical cost falls.
3. **Averages are unsafe verdicts.** National output or household resources
   can improve while a region, cohort or household member carries the loss.
4. **Preparation should begin before certainty.** Forecast certainty often
   arrives after avoidable harm. Reversible, capacity-building preparation can
   be justified under uncertainty when its downside is bounded.
5. **Public legibility matters.** People need to see what changed, what did not,
   why it matters, who can act, who cannot, and what would reverse a conclusion.
6. **Forecast memory is a public good.** Immutable forecasts and corrections
   constrain hindsight, cherry-picking and institutional amnesia.

### Where I would push back

1. **`Abundance` is a mission word, not a measurement.** More output, lower
   marginal cost or greater technical capability can coexist with scarcity of
   housing, care, trust, land, time, power and ecological capacity.
2. **The original master proposition cannot lose.** Saying valuable capability
   can become shared agency is modal. Any failure can be assigned to a missing
   condition. The empirical burden must move to bounded claims with a fixed
   denominator, horizon, comparison and abandonment rule.
3. **Technology is not an external wave.** Ownership, labour law, procurement,
   public investment, market structure and product design shape what is built,
   adopted and counted as valuable. The Observatory must observe this power,
   not only downstream outcomes.
4. **Early warning can create the event.** A public label can change employer,
   worker, market and government behaviour. Warning exposure and downstream
   response are part of the causal system.
5. **Information is not legitimacy.** A perfectly measured threshold does not
   choose the acceptable trade-off, authorise an intervention or create
   consent. Those decisions belong to named people and institutions with
   challenge and exit rights.
6. **One public narrative can become coercive.** Crisis framing may justify
   surveillance, paternalism, emergency drift or a national rule that erases
   local authority. Affected people must be able to reject the metric, goal or
   action, not merely annotate it.
7. **The Observatory itself must earn its complexity.** If a short statistical
   release plus legitimate deliberation produces equal or better decisions at
   lower cost and harm, this product should narrow to a provenance service.

### The strongest defensible position now

> For a named service, cohort, place and period, test whether actual adoption
> improves specified outcomes and voluntary choice without transferring
> material harm, and test whether a provenance-rich evidence interface improves
> decisions relative to simpler public-statistics and deliberation baselines.

That position keeps the optimism. It removes inevitability, vague universality
and implied authority.

## 🧩 The IF Protocol should become the product kernel

The public sentence needs to preserve ordinary language while compiling into a
deterministic record:

> **WHO commits to VERB WHAT, to achieve OUTCOME at STANDARD, for PEOPLE in
> PLACE during PERIOD, PROVIDED ALL enabling conditions hold, using ANY
> authorised alternative route, BLOCKED IF ANY veto holds, reviewed on DATE,
> reversed IF harm exceeds LIMIT, and repaired or compensated IF protected
> outcomes worsen.**

`IF` remains the public concept. Its executable parts must be distinct:

| Public question | Typed logic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| What must all be true? | `all` | Every required predicate must pass |
| Which route is enough? | `any` or `alternative_if` | At least one equivalent route must pass |
| What defeats the route? | `not` or `veto_if` | A blocker pre-empts the result |
| What is not known? | five-valued state | Unknown, missing, stale and conflicted remain distinct |
| Who may decide? | binding record | Observation, recommendation, commitment and authority are separate |
| What changes next? | state transition | Prior state, new evidence, decision effect and reversal are explicit |

`UNLESS` should not remain an executable operator. Ordinary speech uses it for
both substitution and veto, which caused opposite public and machine meanings.
The interface may render plain language, but the stored record must say
`alternative_if` or `veto_if`.

### Minimum condition record

Every material predicate needs:

```text
condition id
scope: actor, people, outcome, standard, place, period
axis: capability, reach, agency, fairness, ecology or other declared axis
observable and unit
direction and threshold
measurement state
predicate truth
evidence grade and dependencies
source vintage and exact input checksum
uncertainty and missingness
owner of the measure
owner of the threshold
people bearing false-positive, false-negative and delay costs
alternative routes
vetoes and no-offset harms
falsifier, review date and expiry
prior state, transition evidence and decision effect
```

Five axes must never be compressed into a generic `state`:

1. **Lifecycle** - proposed, specified, observable, measured, reviewed, retired.
2. **Measurement** - absent, stale, conflicted, partial, current.
3. **Predicate truth** - true, false, unknown, not-applicable, disputed.
4. **Evidence grade** - descriptive, associational, predictive, causal or other
   versioned scale.
5. **Binding** - observation, option, recommendation, commitment, authorised
   action, active delivery or expired.

Conversion between axes requires a versioned rule, a direction and provenance.
No machine should infer that `below threshold` means safe without knowing
whether higher or lower values are desirable.

### Decision precedence

The resolver should make conflicts visible:

```text
if reverse is true                 → reverse
else if pause is true              → pause
else if a hard safeguard is unknown→ blocked_pending_evidence
else if act is true                → eligible_for_authority_review
else if watch is true              → watch
else                               → no_decision
```

`Eligible` is not `authorised`. Authorisation requires a separately verified,
scoped and current action compact. Recovery and graduation occur only after
protected outcomes and residual harms are measured.

## 🚨 Crisis points without crisis theatre

The Observatory should forecast resolvable intermediate failures, not one
grand civilisational event. A crisis point is a crossing where delay becomes
materially costly, preparation capacity is finite and a legitimate response
exists.

| Crossing | Example leading evidence | What must not be inferred | Candidate preparation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capability jump | Cost, quality and task performance change | Reach, adoption or job loss | Measure adoption and redesign capacity |
| Adoption bottleneck | Business use, workflow redesign, infrastructure | Economy-wide productivity | Remove access barriers only if harms are bounded |
| Labour transition breach | Worker flows, hours, earnings, duration | AI causation from employment movement | Expand reversible navigation and income-delivery capacity |
| Essential-access breach | Service-specific household and individual margin | Household average equals individual control | Protect continuity, refusal, appeal and privacy |
| Ownership concentration | Market power, rents, switching and bargaining | Concentration alone proves welfare loss | Prepare competition, interoperability and shared-return options |
| Ecological capacity breach | Local energy, water, minerals, emissions and waste | National averages imply local acceptability | Pause expansion or change siting and design under local authority |
| Governance overload | Review queues, appeal delay, delivery failure | An announced programme is an effective one | Rehearse capacity and narrow commitments |
| Information hazard | Anxiety, stigma, gaming, self-fulfilling response | More attention means better decisions | Reduce signal reach, delay publication or revert to private shadowing |

For each crossing, publish four separate objects:

1. **Observation** - what the source measured and its limitations.
2. **Forecast** - a probability or distribution for a resolvable event, scored
   against a declared baseline.
3. **Decision rule** - the loss-weighted condition under which review becomes
   appropriate.
4. **Action compact** - the authorised and reversible response, including who
   can refuse, appeal, pause and repair harm.

This separation is the best defence against a chart silently becoming a policy.

## 🧭 Preparation at four levels

These are candidate preparations for evaluation, not instructions. Each should
be kept only where the benefits survive several plausible futures and the
burden does not fall on the people with least power.

### Individuals and households

- Preserve portable evidence of skills and work, if privacy and employer power
  are protected.
- Know existing income, housing, health, training and appeal pathways, if the
  information is current and access does not require unnecessary disclosure.
- Build optionality through learning, networks and financial buffers, if the
  advice does not individualise a structural failure or exclude low-income
  households.
- Track desired hours, unpaid care, control, privacy and ability to refuse, not
  only household income.

### Workplaces, unions and communities

- Map task redesign and worker flows before inferring displacement.
- Negotiate notice, consultation, redeployment, benefit-sharing, data limits,
  human review and appeal before adoption becomes irreversible.
- Maintain local service and mutual-support capacity, if governance and data
  rights sit with affected communities.
- Record dissent and alternative goals as decision inputs, not communications
  problems.

### Countries and public institutions

- Measure actual adoption, market structure, labour flows, service access,
  administrative capacity and ecological load with published vintages.
- Stress-test automatic stabilisers, income delivery, training, care, housing,
  health and appeals against plausible demand and error rates.
- Prepare competition, interoperability, worker voice, public-option and
  shared-return mechanisms before concentration becomes difficult to reverse.
- Bind temporary actions to expiry, review, compensation and no-loss-of-
  essential-access exit rights.
- Fund trusted statistics, prospective cohorts and challenge infrastructure
  before funding persuasive prediction interfaces.

### International and federated action

- Publish comparable aggregates while retaining local definitions, raw-data
  sovereignty and Indigenous governance.
- Make cross-border floors minimum protections, not permission regimes.
- Track transferred ecological, labour and supply-chain burdens rather than
  allowing one country's apparent abundance to rest on another's scarcity.
- Coordinate shared observations and forecast scoring without creating a
  supranational authority to choose thresholds for affected populations.

## 🔬 What Round 02 genuinely established

The three reviews reproduced a useful narrow foundation:

- The frozen tag, 28-artifact manifest, deterministic builds and 74 tests were
  reproducible.
- Official NERO archive hashes, shape and aggregate descriptive arithmetic were
  independently reproduced.
- The repository correctly refuses to infer AI adoption, causal displacement
  or crisis from NERO employment movement.
- It correctly records that one current NERO archive cannot support a
  historical as-published detector backtest.
- Unknown global conditions remain unknown, no crisis probability is shown and
  no action is authorised.
- A correction and immutable-review habit now exists.

These are controls, not proof of the thesis or product.

## 🛑 What failed and why it matters

Round 02 contains 24 accepted findings: ten stop-lines, thirteen major findings
and one minor finding. Independent convergence increases priority, not truth.

### 1. Authority can be fabricated

Two reviewers independently made the validator authorise a public artifact
using synthetic or forged self-attestation. The function validates internal
consistency, not identity, authority, evidence bytes or legitimacy.

**Decision:** Rename it as structural eligibility for human review. No release
authorization exists until an external trusted boundary verifies scoped,
signed, expiring and revocable attestations and exact evidence bytes.

### 2. The evidence chain stops at transformed JSON

The artifact hash proves which copied values were rendered. It does not prove
how exact upstream bytes became those values. Live mutable endpoints, missing
raw payload checksums and missing adapter contracts prevent end-to-end
reproduction.

**Decision:** Content-address raw source bytes, response metadata, publisher
vintage, selected fields, licence and adapter version. Rebuild from those
inputs or label the result non-reproducible.

### 3. IF logic can invert the decision

Equivalent alternatives and blocking vetoes share the word `UNLESS`. The
public protocol and evaluator give it opposite meanings. Untyped state axes
and missing cross-gate precedence compound the ambiguity.

**Decision:** Separate `alternative_if` from `veto_if`, publish exhaustive
truth tables, type every state axis and enforce hard safety precedence.

### 4. A valid build can still make a false or unsafe page

Unknown fields containing personal data can be embedded invisibly. Reversed or
misaligned series can create a false headline. Unsafe links can reach `href`.
An active action can omit its authority and appeal.

**Decision:** Build from an allowlisted public projection after strict schema
and semantic validation, then test malicious and malformed inputs before any
file is written.

### 5. The model can erase the person who loses

The Australian pilot may substitute a broader population for the affected
cohort. Household access can improve while an individual loses earnings, time,
privacy, bargaining power or refusal rights. Ecology is named in the promise
but not required by the contract.

**Decision:** Separate cohort from place, household access from individual
agency, and ecological lifecycle assessment from generic durability. Severe
individual or local harms cannot be offset by aggregate gains.

### 6. The proposed validation cannot validate the instrument

Three dashboard layouts do not test the dashboard against simpler alternatives.
The affected-party sample can contain no directly affected participant, and
the 27 of 30 boundary permits three dangerous misunderstandings to pass.

**Decision:** Compare simpler baselines, create a distinct directly affected
stratum, let affected representatives own harm thresholds and use subgroup
uncertainty rather than pooled reassurance.

### 7. Parts of the historical and forecast story overclaim

The Engels' Pause supports a descriptive divergence between output and wages.
It does not establish one universal transition shape or the proposed Factory
Acts causal chain. The NERO plan also uses partially dependent evidence as
independent, invents midnight release timing and compares precision with a
no-alert baseline where precision is undefined.

**Decision:** Keep descriptive history, mark contested mechanisms, encode
source dependence and exact chronology, and compare no-alert through decision
utility rather than undefined precision.

## 🏗️ Round 03 plan of attack

Each slice follows the same loop:

```text
red test or falsification fixture
→ smallest correction
→ focused reproduction
→ full clean reproduction
→ adversarial review against the exact diff
→ disposition update
→ small commit pushed to the central branch
```

No finding closes because its author says it is fixed. A code closure requires
the original attack plus a neighbouring attack to fail. A human, authority or
methods finding remains open until the named independent owner reviews it.

### Slice 1: Public-build safety

**Outputs**

- Strict main and pilot schemas with no undeclared public fields.
- Allowlisted public projection and prohibited-data canary tests.
- HTTPS and source-host validation.
- Ordered, unique and aligned series; declared identifiers; recomputed latest
  values and headline arithmetic.
- Complete authority metadata for any operational action state.
- Conforming HTML shell, contrast and print baseline.

**Exit:** Every reproduced malicious or malformed build fails before output.

### Slice 2: Deterministic IF semantics

**Outputs**

- Separate alternative and veto operators.
- Exhaustive five-valued truth vectors.
- Typed lifecycle, measurement, predicate, evidence and binding axes.
- Versioned direction-aware conversion rules.
- Decision resolver with pause and reverse precedence.

**Exit:** Independent encoders produce identical results for the golden vectors
and cannot activate an action under a hard unknown, pause or reverse.

### Slice 3: Honest thesis and public language

**Outputs**

- Mission, research question and empirical claims labelled separately.
- Engels' Pause narrowed to supported descriptive evidence.
- Ecology required as assessed or explicitly not material.
- Household access and individual agency linked but not offset.
- Observatory evaluation compared with simpler instruments.

**Exit:** A reviewer can name a plausible negative result for every empirical
claim and cannot mistake the mission for a forecast.

### Slice 4: Evidence kernel

**Outputs**

- Content-addressed raw source store or reproducible external archive contract.
- Source request and response metadata, licence, publisher vintage, selection
  contract and adapter version.
- Per-point epistemic class and method.
- Bit-for-bit rebuild test and source-revision diff.

**Exit:** Mutated upstream bytes, changed columns, revised current endpoints and
modelled tails cannot silently appear as measured facts.

### Slice 5: Australia pilot repair

**Outputs**

- Separate cohort and place conditions.
- Dependence and scope-compatibility gates for corroboration.
- Correct NERO eligibility denominators generated from code.
- Verified release timestamps or conservative first-seen intervals.
- Metric-specific baselines and defined no-alert utility.

**Exit:** Simpson's paradox, shared-input agreement, same-day leakage and
no-event cases all fail closed or return a defined non-go decision.

### Slice 6: Governance boundary

**Outputs**

- Current validator renamed as a consistency and review-readiness assessment.
- G0-G10 evidence coverage represented without claiming substantive approval.
- Design for external identity, signature, scope, expiry, revocation and byte
  verification.
- A deployment kill test that cannot be satisfied by repository text alone.

**Exit:** No repository-local fixture can call itself publicly authorised. A
real release remains impossible until the external system and human authorities
exist.

### Slice 7: Human and institutional preregistration

**Outputs**

- Directly affected and analogous participant strata.
- Ethics, compensation, accessibility and Indigenous-scope gates.
- Three-arm instrument comparison.
- Affected-party-owned harmful-misunderstanding and no-offset thresholds.
- Predeclared decision rights, dissent disposition and stopping rules.

**Exit:** Independent ethics, methods, affected-party and governance owners say
the study may begin. Agent approval cannot satisfy this slice.

### Slice 8: No-consequence prospective rehearsal

**Outputs**

- Consecutive source vintages obtained after preregistration.
- Immutable forecasts scored against valid baselines.
- Warning exposure and behavioural response measured.
- Delivery, appeal, reversal and recovery capacity rehearsed without public
  consequence.

**Exit:** The instrument adds decision value within harm and capacity bounds,
or the finding narrows the product.

### Slice 9: Round 03 freeze and hostile re-review

**Outputs**

- Clean checkout reproduction.
- Content-addressed manifest including all original reports and dispositions.
- New immutable tag that does not move Round 02.
- Independent first-pass system, evidence, thesis and affected-party-protocol
  reviews against the new tag.

**Exit:** No unresolved technical stop-line. Human and institutional stop-lines
remain visibly blocking rather than being relabelled as complete.

## 🚪 What we should not do next

- Do not add more headline indicators before the evidence kernel closes.
- Do not publish a transition probability without a real forecast record and
  baseline score.
- Do not turn NERO movement into an AI causal story.
- Do not call internal consistency `release allowed`.
- Do not recruit participants before ethics, affected-party and Indigenous
  governance gates are real.
- Do not interpret agent agreement as democratic legitimacy or expert approval.
- Do not optimise visual persuasion before testing whether a simpler design is
  safer and more useful.

**The next win is not a more impressive dashboard. It is a dashboard that has
earned the right to be believed.**
