# Round 10 plan

**Make the proposed storm criterion executable without mistaking a related
statistic for the people the criterion names.** The [commission](../meta/round-10-commission.md)
sets the scope. Its unresolved choices stay reversible and in the backlog.
The [progress register](round-10-progress.md) records actual work and misses.

## Work order and boundaries

Each lane has its own branch and stacked draft PR against `ren/round-10`.
Source discoveries are reviewed by a different lane before integration.
Labour-statistics, disability/access and household-welfare objections take
priority over agreement among technology enthusiasts. These are agent-applied
perspectives, not invented consultations with human experts or affected people.

## Measurement admission, before arithmetic

1. Confirm exact retained source identity, national scope, reference period,
   vintage, native denominator, model/survey status and licence assessment.
2. Reproduce country coverage from native rows. A global catalogue count does
   not satisfy 40 of this retained set of 50. Missing countries stay missing.
3. Classify what the source actually measures. A proposed category relationship
   is not evidence that the category binds. No modelled stock becomes a
   directly observed transition just because the field is renamed.
4. Compare only explicitly compatible periods and constructs. One vintage does
   not establish twenty-year comparability. Report the denominator needed to
   express a native change in population points, without silently supplying it.
5. Compute the proposed candidate state only when its required evidence is
   admitted. Otherwise return cannot-say, with exact missing evidence. An
   unknown branch in the OR rule cannot silently become false.

The two affected-share readings remain separately labelled: direct disruption
and household exposure. Household exposure must disclose whether it includes
the directly affected person; never add overlapping readings, series, regions
or national percentages. No retained mapping means that reading is unavailable.

## Retrospective challenge

Use twenty adjacent annual transitions ending in 2006-2025, requiring the
retained 2005 baseline. Separate native-indicator movements from actual
criterion fires. Compare against a small retained 2008-2009/2020 shock register
as a mechanics sanity check, not ground-truth labels for every country.
Report unmatched fires, missed benchmark episodes, incomparable intervals and
unevaluable outcomes. Missing evidence is not a miss or a negative outcome.
If all evaluable episodes fire or none do, flag the rule's usefulness for
review; do not tune the threshold on this same history and call it validation.

## IF evolution and prospective learning

Bind each admitted indicator family to an exact existing-family definition,
including its population and category. Do not first register a meaning known
to be false merely to create a correction event. A real correction needs an
existing consumer, a source-backed changed construct, retained old/new
identities, explicit adoption and a regression rejecting its old binding.
New construction, research discoveries and completed migrations are separate
counts. The existing one real event is not relabelled as three.

The third forecast is independent of retrospective selection. Review its
single-cell target, baseline, retained publication calendar, immutable inputs,
resolver and chronology before creating provider issuance evidence. If a
source lacks a defensible resolution window under 120 days, report the gate
unmet. A forecast of a publisher's future estimate must say so, rather than
presenting it as a forecast of people's lived disruption.

## Integration and stopping

Run focused tests first, then independent lane review and the complete Node 22
suite. Inspect intended generated-output changes before updating the artifact
lock; reproduce all producers in CI. Do not treat hashes as authentication.
Keep a decision record for the September 16 receipt-trust review without
granting empirical or public authority through that record.

When gates are met or meaningful in-scope progress is exhausted, freeze a
committed candidate under `review-freeze.round-10`, retaining failures as
failures. Publish an external review brief with the eight established tracks,
splitting measurement into depth, breadth and storm criterion. State every
unmet gate and every process miss. Stop for external review; no main merge or
public release is part of this commission.
