# Decision experience, preparation without invented validation

## 🦅 TL;DR

**We now have a reviewable, runnable decision rehearsal using retained real-world context. We do not yet have evidence that people understand it better or make better choices.** The package mirrors the same facts in two presentation proposals, names competing defensible next steps, and exposes the conditions that prevent recruitment.

This is Ren's proposed design and analysis under the approved programme plan. It does not invent Fernando's views, appoint people, settle ethical or legal questions, contact participants or publish advice.

## 🧭 A concrete decision rehearsal

[Open the materials](../experiments/decision-experience/README.md). The retained US employment-to-population ratio is 59.467% in 2024 and 59.114% in 2025, a change of -0.353 percentage points. The denominator is people aged 15 and over, not all people. The underlying series is an ILO modelled vintage; row-level actual-versus-projected status is not established. These facts are projected from [retained measurements](../signals/countries/income-measurements.v1.json), with exact JSON pointers, source vintage, capture time and raw-body hash in the task pack.

The current [retained storm review](../signals/countries/storm-review.v1.json) is `cannot-say` for this comparison. **A change in an employment stock share is not a count of people whose income-generating route was disrupted.** The rehearsal asks a reader to distinguish those claims, explain the missing evidence and compare bounded research options.

One option is to scope a direct-measurement feasibility check. Another is to defer and monitor the next comparable release. Both can be defensible under different capacity, expected-information and review conditions. A national warning is not supported by these facts. This is an author-proposed judgement about a fictional research decision, not a validated rubric or advice to affected people.

**Inquiry may seek missing evidence; confirmation cannot skip it.** The matched materials separate a research inquiry's capacity, remit and expected-information conditions from the direct-evidence conditions needed to establish disruption. Evidence admission remains a separate reviewed step. A cross-review caught an earlier sentence that accidentally required the confirming evidence before investigation; this revision corrects it in both layouts.

The pages expose feedback for design inspection. **They must not be reused as held-out outcome tasks after a participant has seen the answers.** A confirmatory study requires separately authored, source-bound held-out cases, piloted for interpretability and scored by independent coders.

## ⚖️ What could invalidate the comparison?

1. **More explanation masquerading as a better interface.** Same input bytes do not establish equal exposure. Inspect visible content, reading order, overflow, zoom, assistive output, feedback availability and actual facilitator scripts. A station screenshot may convey authority differently even when words match. That is an outcome to measure, not a parity assumption.
2. **A rigged comparator.** An intentionally weak conventional release would manufacture a product win. Give it a competent plain-language layout and evidence navigation. If it wins, ship the simpler experience or restrict the station to provenance and forecast memory.
3. **An all-or-nothing endpoint hiding the mechanism.** Complete unaided boundary reconstruction is the inherited proposed primary endpoint. Report component errors as well, but specify multiplicity before outcomes. A total score cannot compensate for an increase in dangerous aggregate-to-person inference or false authority.
4. **Facilitation creating dependence.** Group discussion, facilitator effects and participant contamination can break an individual-independent analysis. Decide whether sessions need cluster assignment or modelling, plan facilitator balance and quantify the effective sample before setting power. Do not call four equal allocation weights an adequate design.
5. **A narrow sample laundering public claims.** Define the intended users and tasks. Recruitment feasibility is not representativeness. Accessibility, language and affected-party differences require explicit scope, analysis and claim limits; do not infer sensitive characteristics from proxies.
6. **Researcher certainty deciding what is appropriate.** Some choices depend on values, opportunity cost and authority. Predefine competing defensible answers and code reasoning, not compliance with the programme's preferred remedy. Refusal and a reasoned challenge are legitimate.
7. **Immediate comprehension presented as future benefit.** A task result cannot establish preparation, preserved income access or reduced harm during a real transition. Those require separate observation or evaluation with an appropriate counterfactual before a causal claim.

These are design critiques, not findings from participants. No external statistical guideline or legal conclusion is asserted by this review.

## 🚦 Current blockers, with the next useful work

The [machine-readable summary](../experiments/decision-experience/readiness-summary.json) contains eleven substantive requirement blockers plus the absent external authority verifier. Role names describe required responsibilities, not appointments.

| Block | Next discriminating deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| Accountable human lead | Named, accepted responsibility with scope and resources. |
| Ethics determination | Review the actual tasks, population and harms through the applicable competent process. |
| Privacy, consent and withdrawal | Minimum-data plan, consent, withdrawal, storage, retention, deletion and complaint route. |
| Affected-party governance | Agreed scope, challenge, refusal, interpretation and community rights where relevant. No quota substitutes for governance. |
| Accessible visible parity | Independent captures and findings for both real candidate presentations. |
| Compensation and support | Approved recruitment/compensation and an actual support route. |
| Independent safety monitor | Accepted appointment, pause authority, escalation and restart procedure. |
| Power and worthwhile effect | Decide the minimum useful improvement, endpoint, precision/power, feasible sample and subgroup safety requirements. |
| Missingness and multiplicity | Specify attrition, contamination, clustering, exclusions, contrasts and missing-data treatment before outcomes. |
| Allocation and coding | Validate assignment, concealment, first-exposure control, independent coding and disagreement handling. |
| Preregistration and freeze | Freeze the actual approved candidate, held-out cases and analysis before confirmatory outcomes. |

**The evaluator never converts an agent-entered reference into approval.** A future separately reviewed authority verifier must establish identity, applicable scope, expiry and revocation. Even all eleven self-asserted approvals leave this package blocked. Its successful reproduction exit code is not recruitment readiness.

## 🛑 Harm gates are human procedures, not green checkboxes

The package retains the six exact proposed stop lines from the earlier manifest: urgent irreversible intent; increased dangerous understanding; serious anxiety, stigma, dignity or blame reports; a missed no-real-service/authority notice; critical assistive access failure; and personal data outside the approved minimum. They propose pause and independent review without compensation across strata.

**The existing `dangerous-understanding-increase > 0` line is not yet an operational sequential statistical rule.** The monitor and statistician must decide its observation window, uncertainty, comparison, escalation and restart procedure. Reported harm must not wait for statistical significance, and mean improvement must not erase subgroup harm. None of these procedures is active in the current no-collection rehearsal.
