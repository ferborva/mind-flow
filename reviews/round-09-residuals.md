coverage. The vacuity item is closed by the brief's permitted documentation
choice, not by claiming the sealed evaluator now rejects it. These are internal
results for independent reproduction, not external acceptance.

The distinction in item 8 matters: a passing kernel validation alone is not an
audit pass. The sealed evaluator preserves historical replay. Promoting these
audit rules later requires an explicit versioned contract migration, not an
unannounced reinterpretation of old receipts.
