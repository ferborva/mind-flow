## 🔎 Verification and investment gates

Tests precede behaviour changes. Each lane has its own worktree; mutation review
must not share a mutable checkout with another reviewer. Preserve historical
capture, source, forecast and receipt bytes. Integrate small agent-authored
commits with explicit provenance. Cross-review the actual code and retained
sources, then reproduce from an exact clean candidate.

Technical completion is separate from source validity, forecasting skill,
participant comprehension and real human benefit. Each needs its own evidence.
The prototype must be willing to lose to a simpler statistical release.
