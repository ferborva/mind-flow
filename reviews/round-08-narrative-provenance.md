## Disclaimer counting scope

General boundary sentences and evidence ceilings have different jobs. A
general disclaimer concerns the authority or status of the whole document.
An evidence ceiling limits a particular inference and belongs beside that
inference. The five rewritten drafts each carry one general boundary statement;
local ceilings remain, including the distinction between health insurance and
affordability. A whole-repository under-five-percent gate still requires a
complete public-document inventory and independent classification, including
public guides that this draft pass has not rewritten.
