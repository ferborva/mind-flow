# The strongest objection changed the forecast

Different-owner internal review caught a material defect that passing tests had
missed: the first Round 09 forecast named two different regions. Its structured
scope and resolver selected Central Coast, SA4 102, but the sealed resolution
sentence selected 101. That is not an editorial correction to make in place.
The old record remains visible and inadmissible under current checks; the
[forecast report](round-09-forecast-intake.md) records the prospective replacement.

## Who challenged what

The measurement agent reviewed the forecast lane independently of its author.
It recomputed the 3,092 baseline, 18 of 23 non-negative two-month changes and
19/25 smoothed probability, then inspected the target prose rather than accepting
seven passing issue tests. It later checked the corrected preparation's exact
tuple and nine declared dependency hashes before registration. Post-issue
chronology and retained bytes require their own check.

The editorial agent reviewed measurement scope, permission language and row
semantics. Corrections distinguish PBS subsidy eligibility from permission to
dispense, state specific ABS exclusions, retain multiple sources beside the
price claim, and use table row headers. The coordinator reviewed the deletion
diff against meaning and found two stranded antecedents. Restoring original
context preserved the below-5% result without adding denominator padding.

The measurement and editorial agents also challenged the evolution blocker.
They agreed that appointment-attempt time and appointment-making time are
different constructs, and that replacing a population label is not a set-based
narrowing. That agreement is not proof no possible future contract can express
the changes. It supports the narrower finding: these discoveries cannot satisfy
the commissioned old-binding-fails/new-binding-passes chain using the current
sealed operation set without a new migration design.

These are peers within one commissioned agent team, not the external review
group or independent affected-party consultation.

| Perspective, simulated by agents | Objection | Candidate response and remaining need |

| Disabled reader | Health literacy is not disability access or usable support | Old national health-literacy context stays a proxy. | Low-income household | A lower cap is not a medicine I can afford to obtain | PBS caps and safety-net rules are distinguished from actual transaction price, unmet demand and linked feasible access. A personal bottleneck requires joint, current pathway evidence. |

No patient, clinician, statistician, disabled representative or household was
consulted in this round. These objections identify what real review must test;
they do not substitute for it.

## What would falsify the handoff

- A new source value cannot be reconstructed from the retained body, selector
  and footnote, or a missing category is described as measured.
- A forecast target differs across prose and structured resolution, its receipt
  predates its seal, or scoring becomes possible before eligible October data.
- A copied rule or source-file count is reported as a real improvement in access.
- A deletion changes Fernando's captured stance or adopts the separate PR #19
  interpretation without the capture-to-seed step.
- A current-binding claim is built by joining unrelated state/national proxies.
- A consistency receipt is presented as independent execution or public authority.

The source search also challenges our own convenience: public directory and
dispensing proxies do exist. The [acquisition note](../research/2026-09-10-access-series-acquisition-gaps.md)
separates unacquired proxies from the missing linked access estimand. This round
stops at that honest boundary, not at a claim that useful data is unavailable.
