# Round 03 affected-party protocol amendments

> **AGENT PROPOSAL. NOT APPROVED FOR RECRUITMENT.** This amendment preserves the
> checksum-pinned Round 02 protocol unchanged. It records corrections to apply
> only when a new protocol version is deliberately frozen and reviewed.

## 🦅 Decision first

**Treat 180 participants as a representational coverage floor, not a
confirmatory sample-size calculation.** Before recruitment, a statistician must
increase the total and each relevant arm or perspective cell to meet the
preregistered power or precision requirement for the primary estimand, harm
bound, multiplicity plan and expected attrition.

The six perspective-group minima and cross-cutting access minima remain
coverage safeguards. They do not prove that a between-arm effect or subgroup
harm can be estimated precisely. The final sample must satisfy both:

1. the affected-party and accessibility coverage floor; and
2. the larger prospective statistical requirement.

If either is unmet, the confirmatory gate was not tested. Do not convert an
underpowered null result into equivalence or safety.

## Further bindings before a new freeze

- Bind the exact primary estimand and smallest useful effect.
- Give directly affected participants standing to define unacceptable harms
  and subgroup stop lines before the power analysis.
- Predeclare attrition, clustering, multiplicity and missing-data handling.
- Keep directly affected and analogous participants in separate strata.
- Use `at or above` for any stop boundary intended to trigger at equality.
- Compare the Observatory with the simpler release and facilitated-deliberation
  arms using identical facts, uncertainty, scope and time.

The current experiment design is in the
[Observatory public-instrument comparison](../experiments/observatory-comparison/README.md).
Neither document grants ethics approval, authority to recruit or permission to
publish findings.
